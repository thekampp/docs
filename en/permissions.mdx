---
title: "Roles and permissions"
description: "Define what each team member can see and do in the dashboard."
---

Every person you invite gets a **role**, which already comes with a set of permissions.
After that, if you need to, you adjust permission by permission on that person.

## The roles

| Role | General idea |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | You. Full access, and it can't be changed. |
| **Administrator** | Your right hand: manages almost everything. |
| **Coach** | The day-to-day — athletes, workouts, assessments. It's the invite default. |
| **Assistant** | Operational support, with narrower access. |
| **Analyst** | Focused on following the numbers. |
| **Billing** | Focused on the billing side. |

To change someone's role, use the selector in the **Role** column, in the **Team** table —
the change takes effect right away.

<Note>
  The **Owner** row has no actions menu: the owner can't be demoted or removed from the
  dashboard.
</Note>

## Adjusting one person's permissions

The role is the starting point. To fine-tune it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team">
    **Team** menu, **Members** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the person's menu">
    Click the three dots (⋮) on their row and choose **Permissions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on and off what you need">
    A side panel opens with the permissions grouped. Each one has a toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The button shows how many changes are pending, like **Save (3)**. Without clicking
    **Save**, nothing is applied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Changing the **role** takes effect right away; touching the **permissions** requires
  clicking **Save**. They're two different things on the same screen.
</Warning>

### How to read the panel

- Permissions you changed from the role's default get the **changed** mark.
- Sensitive permissions come with an **amber padlock**.
- The sensitive data group appears **highlighted in amber**, with a shield.

<Note>
  Permissions are **exceptions on top of the role**. That's why changing the person's role
  afterwards makes the new role's default apply again.
</Note>

## What "sensitive" means here

It's not integrations or billing data: it's the **athlete's data** — intake form, health
information, measurements and Body Scan images.

That's why the panel reminds you: **Sensitive data requires the athlete's consent.** The
permission you grant is only half of it; the other half is the athlete having shared that
data. See [Sensitive data and consent](/en/sensitive-data).

<Tip>
  Before you invite someone, the invite modal shows **What a [role] can do** — a preview of
  that role's permissions. Worth reading before you choose.
</Tip>

## Who can change this

The **Permissions** option only shows up for people who have permission to manage
permissions — not every administrator does. And anyone who can't manage the team just sees
the notice *You don't have permission to manage the team.*

<Tip>
  Give each person only the access they actually need. Besides being safer, it keeps their
  dashboard tidier — and every access to sensitive data is recorded in the
  [Audit log](/en/audit-log).
</Tip>
