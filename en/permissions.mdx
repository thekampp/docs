---
title: "Roles and permissions"
description: "Define what each team member can see and do in the dashboard."
---

Each person you invite to the dashboard gets a **role**. The role defines what they can
do — so you give each one the right access, without opening everything to everyone.

## The idea

Think of roles as access levels:

- Those who **manage everything** (you, the dashboard owner): full access, including
  team, integrations, and automations.
- Those who **handle the day-to-day** (trainers): work with athletes, workouts, and
  assessments.
- Those with **more limited** access: view or help with specific parts.

## How to adjust

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team">
    Open the **Team** area in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the member">
    Click the person whose access you want to adjust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the role">
    Select the role that fits their job. Access changes right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Give each person only the access they actually need. It's safer and keeps the
  dashboard tidier.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Some more sensitive areas — like integrations and account data — usually stay with
  whoever manages the dashboard.
</Note>
