---
title: "Connect Sellyo"
description: "Link your Sellyo products to Kampp so access is delivered as soon as the sale is approved."
---

With Sellyo connected, every approved sale creates the buyer's account in Kampp and
triggers delivery. Refunds and chargebacks remove access.

## In Kampp: create the integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In the menu, go to **Integrations** and click the **Sellyo** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new integration">
    Click **New Integration** and give it a name (e.g. "Sellyo — Mentorship"). One
    integration per product keeps logs and intake rules neatly separated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the buyers' password">
    **Set a default password** off = each buyer gets a random password. On = everyone gets
    the password you type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm refund and chargeback">
    Leave **Revoke on refund** and **Revoke on chargeback** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and copy the URL">
    Click **Create Integration** and copy the **Webhook URL**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## In Sellyo: paste the URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open webhooks">
    In the Sellyo dashboard, go to **Integrations → Webhooks → Add Webhook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the product">
    Select the product that unlocks this workout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL and tick the events">
    Add the Kampp URL and select:

    - **Sale - Approved** — unlocks access.
    - **Sale - Refund** — removes it.
    - **Sale - Chargeback** — removes it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Sale - Rejected** and pending payments are recognized but don't unlock access. They
  show up in the logs as **Ignored**.
</Note>

## Test it

After the first sale, open the integration in Kampp and check **Recent logs** — the sale
should appear with **Access granted**. See [track incoming events](/en/webhook-logs) if
something doesn't add up.
