---
title: "Build a diet plan"
description: "Create a meal plan with meals, foods, macro targets and a water goal."
---

The **Diets** menu is where you build meal plans and deliver them to athletes — the same
way you do with workouts. The screen has two tabs: **Templates** (the plans you built) and
**Assigned** (plans already with athletes).

## Create the plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New diet">
    In the menu, go to **Diets** and click **New diet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set the targets">
    In the **Daily targets** block, fill in **Calories**, **Protein**, **Carbs** and
    **Fat**. The dashboard warns you when the macros don't add up to the calories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the days of the week">
    Under **Days of the week**, leave ticked the days this menu applies to. On unticked days
    it simply doesn't show up for the athlete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the water goal">
    Under **Water goal**, enter how many ml per day. That's what feeds the app's water
    counter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the menu">
    Use **Add to menu** to include:

    - **Meal** — breakfast, lunch, a snack… you add foods with amount and unit, and the
      macros add up on their own.
    - **Prescription** — your supplement list (whey, creatine, omega 3). It doesn't count
      toward macros and doesn't become a check-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the nutrition summary">
    On the right, **Nutrition analysis** adds everything up live and shows how much is
    missing (or over) for each macro.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Done. The diet goes into the **Templates** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  When you start from scratch, Kampp already suggests five meals (breakfast, morning snack,
  lunch, afternoon snack and dinner). Rename, remove or add whatever you want.
</Tip>

## Searching foods

When adding an item, search by name ("rice", "chicken", "banana") and choose the **amount**
and the **unit**. The dashboard shows the macros **in that serving**, so you can adjust
without a calculator.

## General guidance

Use the **General guidance** field for what doesn't fit inside a meal: what to do on days
without a menu, how to swap a food, cheat-day rules.

## Organizing in folders

Just like workouts, you can group diets into **folders** (e.g. "Fat loss", "Hypertrophy").
Use **New folder** in the **Templates** tab, and the diet's options menu (⋯) to **Move to
folder**.

The (⋯) menu also has **Edit diet**, **Duplicate diet**, **Assign to athletes** and
**Delete diet**.

<Note>
  Duplicating is the shortcut for variations: build the 2,400 kcal plan, duplicate it and
  adjust to 2,000 kcal instead of starting over.
</Note>
