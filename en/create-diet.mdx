---
title: "Build a diet plan"
description: "Create a meal plan with meals, foods, macro targets and a water goal."
---

The **Diets** menu is where you build meal plans and deliver them to athletes — the same
way you do with workouts. The screen has two tabs: **Templates** (the plans you built) and
**Assigned** (plans already with athletes).

## Create the plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New diet">
    In the menu, go to **Diets** and click **New diet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set the targets">
    In the **Daily targets** block, fill in **Calories**, **Protein**, **Carbs** and
    **Fat**. The dashboard warns you when the macros don't add up to the calories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the days of the week">
    Under **Days of the week**, leave ticked the days this menu applies to. On unticked days
    it simply doesn't show up for the athlete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the water goal">
    Under **Water goal**, enter how many ml per day. That's what feeds the app's water
    counter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the menu">
    Use **Add to menu** to include:

    - **Meal** — breakfast, lunch, a snack… you add foods with amount and unit, and the
      macros add up on their own.
    - **Prescription** — your supplement list (whey, creatine, omega 3). It doesn't count
      toward macros and doesn't become a check-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the nutrition summary">
    On the right, **Nutrition analysis** adds everything up live and shows how much is
    missing (or over) for each macro.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Done. The diet goes into the **Templates** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  When you start from scratch, Kampp already suggests five meals (breakfast, morning snack,
  lunch, afternoon snack and dinner). Rename, remove or add whatever you want.
</Tip>

## Searching foods

When adding an item, search by name ("rice", "chicken", "banana") and choose the **amount**
and the **unit**. The **In this serving** panel shows the macros for that amount, so you can
adjust without a calculator.

Two things that save time:

- **Category filter** — when you can't remember the exact name, browse by category.
- **Green check** — foods you already use in other diets come marked, so it's easy to keep
  your coaching standard.

The **unit** doesn't have to be grams: many foods have ready-made servings (e.g. *slice
(30g)*), which is how the athlete thinks at mealtime.

## Swaps: the "or" in the menu

Not every athlete eats chicken every Monday. To offer an alternative without messing up the
menu, turn an item into a **swap** for the item above it:

- **drag** the item onto the middle of the item it replaces, **or**
- use **Mark as a swap for the item above**.

The item becomes a child of the first one and shows up indented, preceded by an italic *or*.

<Tip>
  Swaps are the feature that most cuts down on "can I swap X for Y?" messages. Leave an
  alternative on the main proteins and carbs and you'll save a lot of chat.
</Tip>

## Meal time

Each meal takes a **time** (optional). Besides organizing the menu, it's what feeds the
**reminders** in the athlete's app.

## While you build

- **Automatic draft** — if you close midway, when you come back Kampp tells you *Draft
  recovered — picking up where you left off* and you can **Discard** if you want to start
  over.
- **Save draft** stores it without putting it into use; **Save template** publishes it to your
  library.
- If the macros don't add up to the calories, Kampp tells you how much is over or missing so
  you can adjust.

## General guidance

Use the **General guidance** field for what doesn't fit inside a meal: what to do on days
without a menu, how to swap a food, cheat-day rules.

## Organizing in folders

Just like workouts, you can group diets into **folders** (e.g. "Fat loss", "Hypertrophy").
Use **New folder** in the **Templates** tab, and the diet's options menu (⋯) to **Move to
folder**.

Each folder has a **name** and a **color**, opens and closes, and shows how many diets are
inside. Diets without a folder stay in the **My diets** group.

<Note>
  Deleting a folder **does not delete the diets** — they go back to **My diets**.
</Note>

In the (⋯) menu you'll also find **Edit diet**, **Use this one**, **Duplicate diet**, **Assign
to athletes** and **Delete diet**.

**Use this one** sends a copy of the diet to **your** own diary in the app, for you to follow
— without affecting any athlete.

<Note>
  Duplicating is the shortcut for variations: build the 2,400 kcal plan, duplicate it and
  adjust to 2,000 kcal instead of starting over.
</Note>
