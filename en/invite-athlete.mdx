---
title: "Invite a client to your team"
description: "Bring your client into your team by sending a simple link."
---

The fastest way to start following someone is to invite that person to a **team**.
You copy a link, send it to your client (over WhatsApp, for example), and they join
on their own — no manual sign-up needed from you.

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your team">
    In the side menu, go to **Teams** and open the team you want to invite the client
    to. If you don't have a team yet, create one — by class, by sport, or however
    makes sense for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the invite link">
    Inside the team, click **Copy invite link**. Done — the link is now on your
    clipboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to your client">
    Paste the link wherever is easiest for the person — WhatsApp, email, DM. The same
    link works for many clients, so feel free to reuse it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your client joins on their own">
    When they open the link on their phone, the client downloads (or opens) the Kampp
    app and joins your team directly. From there, they show up in your client list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the client sees

When you send the link, it shows up with a friendly preview, something like
**"You've been invited to the team \[your team's name]"** — so the person immediately
understands it's an invite from you, not just some random link.

<Tip>
  Want to invite a whole class? Use the **same link** for everyone. Each person who
  opens it joins your team individually.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The client uses the mobile app to train and track everything. You, as the coach,
  manage it all from here, in the dashboard.
</Note>

## What's next?

With the client on your team, you can already **send an intake form**, **record a
physical assessment**, and **build their workouts**. Each of these has its own
