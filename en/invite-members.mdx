---
title: "Invite members to your dashboard"
description: "Bring in other professionals to help you manage your clients."
---

If you work with other people — another trainer, an intern, someone at the front desk —
you can invite them to your dashboard. That way, more than one person helps look after
the clients, each with their own access.

## How to invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team">
    Open the **Team** (members) area in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the member">
    Click **Invite member** and enter the person's email. You also set their role (what
    they'll be able to do).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The person accepts">
    The invitee receives the invite and, once they accept, gets access to the dashboard
    with the role you chose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Each member signs in with their own account. You don't share your password — everyone
  has their own access.
</Tip>

<Note>
  What each member can see and do depends on the **role** (permissions) you give them.
  See the "Roles and permissions" tutorial.
</Note>
