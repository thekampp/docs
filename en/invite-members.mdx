---
title: "Invite members to your dashboard"
description: "Bring in other professionals to help you manage your athletes."
---

If you work with other people — another trainer, an intern, someone at the front desk —
you can invite them to your dashboard. That way, more than one person helps look after
the athletes, each with their own access.

## How to invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team">
    Open the **Team** (members) area in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the member">
    Click **Invite member** and enter the person's **email** and their **role** (the
    default is **Coach**).

    When you pick the role, a preview appears — **What a [role] can do**. It's worth
    reading before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The person accepts">
    They get an email and, once they accept, get access to the dashboard with the role you
    chose. Until then, they show up on the list as **Invite pending**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How many people fit

Above the table you'll see **Seats: X / Y** — how many you already use and how many your
plan allows. If you run out, the invite isn't sent and you'll need to change plans. See
[Plan and subscription](/en/plan-subscription).

## Managing who's already in

The (⋮) menu on each member gives you **Permissions**, **Suspend** (or **Activate**) and
**Remove**. In the **Role** column, the picker changes their role right away.

The possible statuses are **Active**, **Invite pending**, **Suspended** and **Removed**.

<Tip>
  Each member signs in with their own account. You don't share your password — everyone
  has their own access.
</Tip>

<Note>
  What each member can see and do depends on the **role** and the permissions you set. See
  [Roles and permissions](/en/permissions).
</Note>

<Tip>
  Everything your team does — including who opened an athlete's sensitive data — is logged.
  See [Audit](/en/audit-log).
</Tip>

<Note>
  On the other side, whoever received the invite can follow

</Note>
