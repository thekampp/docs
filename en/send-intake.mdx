---
title: "Send an intake form to an athlete"
description: "Send a questionnaire from your library to a specific athlete to answer in the app."
---

Besides the automatic rules, you can send an intake form **right now** to a specific
athlete — when they're back from an injury, changed goals, or you just want fresh data.

## How to send it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the athlete">
    In **Athletes**, open the person's profile and go to the **Assessments** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send intake form">
    In the **Intake** block, use the **Send intake form** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick which form">
    Select a questionnaire from your library and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They answer in the app">
    They get a notification and answer right in the app. The answers come back to this
    same screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the list is empty, you haven't created any intake form yet. Build one in
  **Settings → Intake** — see [create an intake form](/en/create-intake).
</Note>

## Seeing the answers

The **Intake** block in the profile holds every form for that athlete, newest first:

- **Pending** (clock) — sent, not answered yet.
- **Answered** (check) — click to open the answers, photos included.

<Tip>
  Sending the same form again creates a **new round**. The old answers stay, so you can
  compare before and after.
</Tip>

## Manual sending doesn't lock workouts

A manually sent form is a request, not a requirement: the athlete keeps normal access to
their workouts.

If you want the workout to unlock only after the answer, use a rule in
**Settings → Intake → When to require**. See
[when to require the intake](/en/recurring-intake).
