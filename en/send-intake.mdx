---
title: "Send the intake form for the client to fill in"
description: "Generate a link and let the client answer the intake form from their phone."
---

The intake form is that initial questionnaire — health history, habits, goals.
Instead of filling it all in by hand, you can **send a link** and let the client
answer it, right from their phone.

## How to send it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client">
    In **Clients**, open the person's profile and go to the **Assessments** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose to send the intake">
    Click to **send the intake form** and generate a link for the client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what to ask">
    Select which fields you want the client to answer. There's a suggested default set
    — add or remove whatever you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    Copy the link and send it over WhatsApp (or wherever you prefer). The client opens
    it, answers, and the responses come straight back to their profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You choose which questions to send each client. For some, just the basics; for
  others, something more complete. It's flexible.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Some fields are health-related (conditions, medications, readiness screening). Handle
  this information carefully — it's sensitive client data.
</Note>
