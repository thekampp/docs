---
title: "Turn the suggestion on for an intake form"
description: "Choose which questionnaires Cortex gets to work on and what it should suggest."
---

The Cortex suggestion **is born from the intake form**. You don't click "generate": you
flip a switch on a questionnaire and, every time an athlete answers it, the suggestion
goes into the queue for you to review.

## Where to turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the intake form">
    Go to **Forms**, **Intake forms** tab, and open the form you want to use (or create a
    new one).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll down to Generate suggestion with AI">
    The block sits after the questions, the completion screen and the delivery rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Flip the switch">
    Turn on **Generate suggestion with AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what should be suggested">
    In the **What should be suggested?** field, pick one of the options:

    - **Use the Coach DNA scope** — follows what you switched on under "What do you
      prescribe?". It's the default and the most common choice.
    - **Workout only**
    - **Diet only**
    - **Workout and diet**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Done. In your intake form list, that form now shows a red **AI** badge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## It lives alongside folder delivery

Turning the suggestion on **doesn't turn off** your delivery rules. The folders you set up
in [Deliver the workout based on the answer](/en/delivery-by-answer) keep working just as
they did.

You can:

- use **only the fixed folder** (the way you always have);
- use **only the suggestion**;
- use **both at once** — the athlete gets the folder right away and you deliver the
  suggestion after reviewing it.

## If the switch is disabled

The toggle stays locked until your **Coach DNA** is ready. The amber notice tells you
what's missing — usually one of these three things:

- describing your methodology (at least 200 characters);
- choosing workout, diet or both;
- activating Coach DNA.

Use the **Set up Coach DNA** link to fix it on the spot.

<Warning>
  If your Coach DNA goes offline after you've already turned the AI on, the suggestions
  **pause**. The athletes' answers sit waiting and generation happens as soon as Coach DNA
  is active again — you don't lose a single answer.
</Warning>

## What now?

When the first athlete answers, the suggestion shows up in **Cortex → Suggestions** with
the **Review** status, and the red badge in the menu lights up.

Head to [Review and deliver a suggestion](/en/cortex-review-suggestion).
