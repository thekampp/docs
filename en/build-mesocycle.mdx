---
title: "Build a mesocycle (periodization)"
description: "Organize phases across the weeks, each phase with its own folder of workouts."
---

A **mesocycle** is a training plan split into phases across a few weeks — the classic
periodization. Instead of sending loose workouts, you design a journey: adaptation,
gains, peak, and so on. Each **phase** of the mesocycle is a **folder** of workouts.

## How to build one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start building the program">
    On the athlete's page, use **Build program** to start the periodization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the phases">
    Split the period into phases (for example: adaptation, hypertrophy, strength). For
    each phase, choose the **folder** with that stage's workouts and set how many
    **weeks** it lasts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the order">
    Place the phases in the sequence that makes sense for the athlete's journey — one
    after another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate it for the athlete">
    With the plan ready, the athlete receives the **current phase** and moves through
    phase after phase, in the order you built.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Since each phase is a folder, it pays off to keep your workout folders well organized
  before building the program — that way you just pick the right folder for each stage.
</Tip>

<Note>
  As time goes by, the phases switch on their own: when one phase ends, the athlete
  automatically receives the next folder's workouts.
</Note>
