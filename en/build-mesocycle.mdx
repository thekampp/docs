---
title: "Build a mesocycle (periodization)"
description: "Organize workouts into phases across the weeks for planned progress."
---

A **mesocycle** is a training plan split into phases across a few weeks — the classic
periodization. Instead of sending loose workouts, you design a journey: adaptation,
gains, peak, and so on.

## How to build one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new mesocycle">
    Open the periodization area and create a **new mesocycle** for the client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the phases">
    Split the period into phases (for example: adaptation, hypertrophy, strength). For
    each phase, set how many weeks it lasts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the workouts in each phase">
    Match the right workouts to each phase, at the intensity that makes sense for that
    moment of the journey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate it for the client">
    With the plan ready, it guides the client phase after phase, in the order you
    built.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Think of the mesocycle as the "map" of the client's progress. The workouts are the
  stops; the mesocycle is the full route.
</Tip>

<Note>
  As time goes by, the client moves through the phases automatically, following the
  plan you designed.
</Note>
