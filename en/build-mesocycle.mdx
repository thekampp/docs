---
title: "Build a mesocycle (periodization)"
description: "Organize phases across the weeks, each phase with its own folder of workouts."
---

A **mesocycle** is a training plan split into phases across a few weeks — the classic
periodization. Instead of sending loose workouts, you design a journey: adaptation,
gains, peak, and so on. Each **phase** of the mesocycle is a **folder** of workouts.

<Note>
  On screen, Kampp calls this a **program**: the button is **Build program**, the field is
  **Program name** and the final button is **Create program**.
</Note>

## How to build one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Build program">
    On the athlete's page, use **Build program**. You can build one for a **team** too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a start date">
    Fill in the **Program name** and the **Starts on** date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the phases">
    Split the period into phases (for example: adaptation, hypertrophy, strength). For each
    phase, choose the **folder** with that stage's workouts and how many **weeks** it lasts
    (up to **24 weeks** per phase).

    The picker shows each folder with how many workouts are inside. If there's no folder with
    workouts, Kampp tells you to create the folders in your library first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the order">
    Use the **↑ ↓** arrows in each phase's header to change the sequence, and the trash icon
    to remove a phase. At the top, the counter shows **how many phases** and **how many weeks
    in total**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create program**. There's no activation step: once you save, it's live — the
    phases switch on their own over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  When you **edit** a program, swapping a phase's folder **replaces that phase's workouts**.
  To adjust a single workout without swapping the whole folder, click it on the program card.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Since each phase is a folder, it pays off to keep your workout folders well organized
  before building the program — that way you just pick the right folder for each stage.
</Tip>

<Note>
  As time goes by, the phases switch on their own: when one phase ends, the athlete
  automatically receives the next folder's workouts.
</Note>
