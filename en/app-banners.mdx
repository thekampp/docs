---
title: "Banners in your athletes' app"
description: "Put banners on the home screen of your athletes' app, choose who sees them and track clicks."
---

Banners show up in the carousel on the **app home screen** of your athletes. Use them to promote an
offer, a challenge, a live session, a new product.

## Create a banner

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Banners">
    In the panel menu, click **Banners**, then **Novo banner** (New banner).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the image or video">
    Upload an image or pick one from your media library. It opens a crop already in the app's
    banner format (**16:9**): drag and zoom. The ideal size is **1600 × 900**. GIFs go in as they
    are, without cropping (up to 20 MB).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Give it a name (only you see it), add a link (optional, opens when the athlete taps) and, if you
    want, start and end dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who sees it">
    With no condition, all your athletes see it. Click **Adicionar condição** (add condition) and pick
    **Entrega** (delivery: "is in" / "is not in") or **Tag** ("has" / "doesn't have"). To add another,
    use **E também** (and also): the athlete must match all of them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Selling an upsell? Use **"is not in"** with the upsell delivery: whoever already bought it
  stops seeing the banner.
</Tip>

## Order and numbers

- Use the arrows to change the order. In the app, Kampp banners come before yours.
- Turn each banner on and off with its switch (the panel asks you to confirm).
- **Duplicate** creates a copy that starts **turned off**, so you can adjust it before going live.
- Each banner shows **impressions** (times seen), **reach** (how many people saw it), **clicks**,
  **CTR** (clicks ÷ impressions) and the **sales** that came from it. The chart shows it by day,
  for the period picked at the top.

## A/B test

Put several versions of a banner in the same carousel spot and see which one brings more clicks and
sales.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the test">
    Click **Novo teste A/B** (new A/B test), or the test icon on an existing banner (it becomes the
    first version). The **audience** belongs to the test and applies to every version.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add versions">
    Use **Nova versão** (new version) or duplicate a version (the copy starts turned off).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Split the traffic">
    Each version has a **%**. Every time the athlete opens the app, one version shows up, drawn by
    each one's %. The percentages must add up to 100. **Dividir igual** (split evenly) shares it
    equally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each version shows the same numbers as a banner, plus **conversion** (sales ÷ clicks). A sale counts
for the version the athlete tapped. Compare the versions and pick the one that works best for you:
turn off the others, or delete the test and keep only the banner you chose.

Pausing the test removes every version from the app.

## Moderation

Kampp may turn off a banner that doesn't follow the usage rules. A banner turned off by Kampp shows
**"Desativado pelo Kampp"** (turned off by Kampp) and can't be turned back on. In repeated cases,
banners may be turned off for the whole account.

<Note>
  On your team, whoever has the **Gerenciar banners do app** (manage app banners) permission can edit
  banners.
</Note>
