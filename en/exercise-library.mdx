The library is your collection of exercises — it's where you pull movements from when
building a workout. Besides the ready-made exercises, you can create your own.

## Add your own exercise

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the library">
    In the **Workouts** area, go to the exercise library and click **Add exercise**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and describe it">
    Name the exercise and, if you want, add execution guidance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a video or image">
    Attach a short demo video (or an image). This helps the athlete execute it right,
    with no doubt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The exercise now shows up in searches when you're building workouts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Recording your own execution videos makes the experience truly yours and gives the
  athlete more confidence.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The exercises you create stay in your library and can be used in as many workouts as
  you want.
</Note>
