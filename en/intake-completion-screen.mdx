---
title: "Intake completion screen (offer and video)"
description: "Show a video, an image and a button when the athlete finishes — and change the screen based on their answer."
---

When the athlete finishes the intake form, they see a completion screen. By default it's
just "Intake form submitted" — but you can turn that moment into your pitch, an offer or
an invitation.

It's the best moment to talk to them: they just answered, they're engaged, and the phone
is in their hand.

## Build the completion screen

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In **Settings → Intake**, open the form and scroll to **Completion screen (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the media">
    Pick **Image** or **Video**.

    - **Image** — you upload the file.
    - **Video** — paste the embed code (the `<iframe>`) from YouTube, Vimeo or SmartPlayer.
      The video is shown in its original format, landscape or portrait.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the copy">
    Fill in **Title** and **Subtitle** (both optional).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the button">
    Tick **Add action button** and fill in the **button text** and the **link** (e.g. a
    coaching page, a group, a product).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save intake form**. The preview on the right shows how it looks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controlling the pace

Two settings stop people from closing before they see it:

- **Hold the close (✕)** — the close button only appears after X seconds.
- **Delay the button** — the action button only appears after X seconds (useful when the
  video needs to be watched before the offer).

<Tip>
  If your video already has its own button inside it, leave the screen **without a button**:
  the athlete closes with the ✕ and all the attention stays on the video.
</Tip>

## Decline screen (downsell)

You can add a second screen shown **when the athlete declines the offer**. Turn on
**Decline screen (downsell)** and build it the same way: media, title, subtitle and button.

The "no thanks" text is yours too — you can change it to something like "Not now, thanks"
or "Just the workout, please".

With no downsell configured, declining simply ends the form.

## A different screen per answer

Want to show one video to people chasing fat loss and another to people chasing
hypertrophy? Use **Completion screen by answer (optional)**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn the block on">
    Scroll to **Completion screen by answer (optional)** and click **Enable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the key question">
    Select the question that decides the screen — it has to be **single choice**, **cards**
    or **multiple choice**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the variants">
    In each **Variant**, tick the answers it applies to and build the screen (media, copy,
    button, downsell).
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the athlete's answer matches a variant, it **replaces** the default completion screen.
If it doesn't match any, they see the default one.

<Note>
  You need at least one choice question in the form to use this — the dashboard tells you
  when there isn't one yet.
</Note>
