---
title: "Fill in the intake form yourself"
description: "Prefer to record answers on the spot? Fill in the intake right in the dashboard."
---

Sending the link isn't always the best move. During an in-person assessment, for
example, it's handier to record the answers yourself while talking to the client. You
can fill in the intake form right in the dashboard.

## How to fill it in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client">
    In **Clients**, open the profile and go to the **Assessments** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the intake">
    Access the client's intake form to edit it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in by section">
    The questions come organized in sections (medical history, habits, training,
    goals…). Fill in what you have — you don't need to answer everything at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The answers are stored in the profile, and you can come back and update them
    whenever you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can start the intake now and finish later. Whatever you save stays there, waiting
  for you to wrap it up.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Mixed the methods? No problem: you can send the link for the client to answer part of
  it and fill in the rest yourself. It all lands in the same place.
</Note>
