---
title: "Athlete birthday message"
description: "Send a happy birthday automatically on each athlete's birthday."
---

Remembering each athlete's birthday is a small gesture that creates a huge amount of
goodwill — and Kampp does it for you, every year, automatically.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the automation">
    In **Kampp Flow**, create an automation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the birthday trigger">
    In the trigger, select **"Athlete's birthday"**. It fires on the right day, using
    the athlete's date of birth on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the birthday wish">
    Add the WhatsApp message, for example: *"Happy birthday, {{name}}! 🎉 May your new
    year be full of health and great workouts!"*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate it">
    Done. From now on, each athlete gets a birthday message on their day, without you
    having to remember.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Consider attaching a celebratory image to make the message even more special (see
  "Attach an image, audio, video, or document").
</Tip>

<Note>
  For this to work, the athlete needs a **date of birth** on file. Without it, Kampp has
  no way to know the date and simply won't send.
</Note>
