---
title: "Send a WhatsApp message in an automation"
description: "Write the message, personalize it with the client's name, and choose how to send it."
---

The most-used Kampp Flow action is sending a WhatsApp message. Here's how to get your
message just right.

## Building the message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the WhatsApp action">
    Inside the automation, click **Add** and choose **Send WhatsApp**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to send it">
    In the **Send via** field, select which of your WhatsApp connections will send the
    message. (You connect WhatsApp once in Integrations.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Type the text. Use **{{name}}** wherever you want the client's name to appear —
    Kampp swaps it in when sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Personalization with {{name}}

Writing *"Hi {{name}}, all good?"* makes each client receive *"Hi Maria, all good?"*,
*"Hi John, all good?"*, and so on. Simple and personal.

<Tip>
  Short, kind messages work best. Talk the way you'd talk to the client in person.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The message is only sent to clients with a phone on file who haven't opted out of
  WhatsApp. If a phone is missing, Kampp simply skips that client, with no error.
</Note>
