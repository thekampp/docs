---
title: "Connect Hotmart"
description: "Link your Hotmart products to Kampp so access is delivered as soon as the purchase is approved."
---

With Hotmart connected, every approved purchase creates the buyer's account in Kampp and
triggers delivery. Refunds, chargebacks and cancellations remove access.

## In Kampp: create the integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In the menu, go to **Integrations** and click the **Hotmart** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new integration">
    Click **New Integration** and give it a name (e.g. "Hotmart — 12-week coaching"). It's
    worth creating one per product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the buyers' password">
    **Set a default password** off = a random password per buyer. On = everyone gets the
    same password you choose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm refund and chargeback">
    Leave **Revoke on refund** and **Revoke on chargeback** on so access is removed
    automatically in those cases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and copy the URL">
    Click **Create Integration** and copy the **Webhook URL**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## In Hotmart: paste the URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open webhooks">
    In the Hotmart dashboard, go to **Tools → Webhooks → Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the Kampp URL">
    Add the URL you copied and select the matching product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the events">
    Tick the purchase events:

    - **Purchase approved** (`PURCHASE_APPROVED`) — unlocks access.
    - **Refund** (`PURCHASE_REFUNDED`) — removes it.
    - **Chargeback** (`PURCHASE_CHARGEBACK`) — removes it.
    - **Cancellation** (`PURCHASE_CANCELED`) — removes it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Generated bank slips and Pix, late payments and abandoned carts are recognized but **do
  not unlock access** — only an approved purchase does. They show up in the logs as
  **Ignored**.
</Note>

## Test it

After the first sale (or a test), open the integration in Kampp: **Recent logs** should
show the purchase with the **Access granted** tag. If something fails, see
[track incoming events](/en/webhook-logs).
