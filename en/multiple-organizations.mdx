---
title: "Work in more than one account"
description: "When you belong to more than one operation, switch between them without changing logins."
---

Some coaches have their own dashboard **and** are part of another company's team. That's
what the **organization switcher** is for.

## Where it is

At the **top of the sidebar**, right below the Kampp logo. It shows the name of the account
you're in and, underneath, **Your organization** (when you're the owner) or your **role**
in that account.

<Note>
  **It only appears if you belong to two or more organizations.** If you only have your own
  dashboard, the switcher isn't there — and that's fine.
</Note>

## How to switch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the switcher">
    It opens the **Switch organization** list, with the current account marked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the other account">
    The whole page reloads.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What changes when you switch

Pretty much everything. Each organization is its own world:

| Separate per organization | Shared |
| --- | --- |
| Athletes, teams, workouts and diets | Your login (same email and password) |
| Team members and permissions | |
| Plan, subscription and seats | |
| Chat, Flow and integrations | |
| Audit log | |

**Your permissions change too.** In your own dashboard you're the owner; in the other
company you might be just a Coach or an Analyst — and then the sidebar shows fewer items.

<Warning>
  Before creating an athlete, building a workout or sending an announcement, **check which
  account you're in**. It's the most common mistake for people working in two operations.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  The account you pick is remembered, and you come back to it next time you sign in. No need
  to log out and back in to switch.
</Tip>
