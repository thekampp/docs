---
title: "Sensitive data and consent"
description: "How Kampp protects an athlete's delicate data — and why sometimes you can't see it."
---

Not every piece of athlete data is the same. Weight and workouts are one thing; body
images and health history are another.

That's why Kampp treats some data as **sensitive**: for you to see it, **two conditions
have to hold at the same time**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The athlete consented">
    They need to have shared that data with the team, from the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your profile has permission">
    Team members only see what their permission allows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where this shows up

Open the athlete → **Assessments** tab → **Sharing** sub-tab.

At the top, the notice: **Sensitive data requires the athlete's consent.**

Today the available block is **Body Scan**, listing the most recent scans with date, waist
and weight. Each row has an **Images** button that opens the scan photos.

## The messages you may run into

| What shows up | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Images locked** (padlock) | Your profile doesn't have that permission. Talk to the account owner. |
| **Your profile doesn't have permission to view this data.** | Same thing — it's a matter of [permissions](/en/permissions). |
| **This athlete hasn't shared this data with the team yet.** | Only the athlete can solve it, from their app. |
| **No Body Scan available.** | They haven't taken a scan yet. |

<Warning>
  There's no button for you to grant consent from the dashboard — and that's on purpose.
  The data belongs to the athlete; they're the one who authorizes it, in the app. If you
  need it, ask them.
</Warning>

## Why this is good for you

It looks like red tape, but it works in your favor:

- **It protects the athlete**, who trusted you with their body.
- **It protects you**, because access to this kind of data is logged — see
  [Audit log](/en/audit-log).
- **It protects your team**, because you control who in your operation sees what.

<Note>
  Want to give or remove someone on your team access to this kind of data? You do it in
  **Team → member menu → Permissions**, in the group highlighted in amber. See
  [Permissions](/en/permissions).
</Note>
