---
title: "Record a physical assessment"
description: "Log measurements, skinfolds, and photos to track the athlete's progress."
---

The physical assessment is where you record the athlete's numbers at a point in time:
weight, measurements, skinfolds, photos. Each assessment is saved with its date, so
over time you build a progress history.

## How to record one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the athlete">
    In **Athletes**, open the profile and go to the **Assessments** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new assessment">
    Click for a **new assessment** and choose the date it was done (today or a past
    date).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the data">
    Record what you measured, by section:
    - **Anthropometry:** weight, height, body fat %.
    - **Girths:** circumferences (waist, arm, thigh…).
    - **Skinfolds:** if you use calipers.
    - **Photos:** front, side, and back, to compare visual progress.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The assessment goes into the athlete's history, ready to compare with the next
    ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  No need to fill in everything. Record only what you use in your assessment — leave
  the rest blank.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Assessment photos are the athlete's personal information. Store and share them
  responsibly.
</Note>

## Let the athlete fill it in

You don't have to measure everything in person. You can generate a link, choose which
fields they fill in and get the data straight into your dashboard — see
[Let the athlete fill in the assessment](/en/assessment-link).
