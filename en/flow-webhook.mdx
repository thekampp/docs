---
title: "Notify another system (Webhook)"
description: "Send the athlete's data and the message that went out to n8n, Make, Zapier or your CRM."
---

The **Webhook** step notifies another system every time the flow reaches it. A common use:
place it right after a WhatsApp step to log the message in your support inbox (Chatwoot,
CRM, spreadsheet...).

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the step">
    In the flow, click **+** and choose **Webhook**. Place it after the message you want to log.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the URL">
    It's the address your automation gives you (for example, the Webhook node in n8n).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to send">
    Tick the data you want to receive. The options follow the message above: Official WhatsApp
    sends the **template name**; z-API and chat send the **message text**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Click **Testar envio** (Send test). You'll get an example with a sample athlete and `"test": true`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you receive

```json
{
  "event": "flow.webhook",
  "test": false,
  "firedAt": "2026-10-22T18:30:05.000Z",
  "executionId": "…",
  "flow": { "id": "…", "name": "Welcome" },
  "athlete": { "id": "…", "name": "Ana Souza", "email": "ana@email.com", "phone": "5511999999999" },
  "message": {
    "channel": "whatsapp_official",
    "status": "success",
    "detail": "template \"welcome\" enviado",
    "sentAt": "2026-10-22T18:30:00.000Z",
    "sentAtBrasilia": "22/10/2026 15:30:00",
    "templateName": "welcome",
    "language": "en_US",
    "variables": ["Ana"]
  }
}
```

- `message` describes the **last message before the webhook**. With no message before it, it's left out.
- `channel`: `whatsapp_official`, `whatsapp_zapi` or `chat`. For z-API and chat, instead of the
  template you get `messages`: a list with `text`, `mediaUrl` and `fileName`.
- `status`: `success` (sent), `skipped` (not sent, for example the athlete has no phone or opted
  out) or `failed`. When it wasn't sent, content and time are left out.

<Tip>
  In your automation, filter on `message.status = "success"` so you don't log a message that
  never reached the athlete.
</Tip>

## Secret key (optional)

With a key, every request carries the header `X-Kampp-Signature: sha256=<signature>`, an
HMAC-SHA256 of the body using your key. Check it to make sure the request came from Kampp.

## If your system is down

If the URL doesn't answer (or returns 5xx or 429), Kampp retries after 1, 5 and 15 minutes.
A 4xx error (wrong address, for example) isn't retried: it shows up in the flow history.
The WhatsApp message before it is **not** sent again.

<Note>
  The URL must be public (`https://`). Internal network addresses are refused.
</Note>
