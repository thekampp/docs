---
title: "Waits and sequences (multiple steps)"
description: "Chain messages with pauses in between to create a sequence across several days."
---

An automation doesn't have to be a single step. You can chain several, with **waits**
in between — creating a sequence that unfolds over the days.

## How to create a sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the first step">
    For example, a welcome message right at the start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a wait">
    Click **Add** and choose **Wait**. Set the time: wait X minutes, hours, or days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the next step">
    After the wait, add another message. And you can repeat: message, wait, message,
    wait…
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two kinds of wait

- **By duration:** wait an amount of time (e.g., 3 days).
- **Until a date:** hold the sequence until a specific date and time (e.g., continue
  only on January 1st).

## Example welcome sequence

1. Client joins the team → welcome message
2. Wait 1 day → "how was your first workout?"
3. Wait 3 days → a tip or support material

<Tip>
  The order of the steps matters — they run top to bottom. You can reorder the steps
  whenever you want.
</Tip>
