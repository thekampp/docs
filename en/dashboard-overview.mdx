When you sign in to Kampp you land on the **Dashboard**: the summary of how your work is
going in the period you chose.

## Choosing the period

The date filter sits in the top right corner. Click it and pick a shortcut — **Today, Last
7 days, Last 14 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month, This year, Last year** or **All
time**.

For a specific range, click the start date and the end date on the calendar and confirm
with **Apply**.

<Tip>
  Your choice is saved: when you come back to the dashboard later, it opens on the same
  period. No need to set it up every time.
</Tip>

## The three numbers at the top

| Card | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Athletes** | The total number of athletes linked to you. |
| **Athletes Training** | How many completed at least one workout in the period. |
| **Completed Workouts** | How many workouts were finished in the period. |

Each card carries a percentage comparing it with the **previous period of the same
length** — the last 7 days against the 7 days before them, and so on.

<Note>
  The **Athletes** card shows your entire roster and **does not change** with the period
  filter.
</Note>

## Athlete activity

A bar chart by day, with **Athletes training** and **Workout completed**. It's there so you
can see the rhythm: strong days, dead days, and whether the whole week is dropping off.

## Alert: athletes who need attention

The most useful card on the screen. It lists who stopped training, sorted by severity:

| Level | When it shows up |
| --- | --- |
| **Attention** | 7 to 13 days without training |
| **At risk** | 14 to 20 days without training |
| **Critical** | 21 days or more without training |

The table shows the first five; use **See all** to open the full list. Clicking any row
takes you straight to that person's profile.

<Warning>
  This card **ignores the period filter** — it always shows who is inactive **right now**.
  The same goes for **Latest activity**. That's on purpose: churn risk is information about
  the present, not about the range you picked.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Treat this list as your task for the day. An athlete on **Attention** still comes back
  with a message; one on **Critical** has usually already decided to leave.
</Tip>

## Earnings report

Shows how much you generated in the period, split between **Athletes** and **Partners**.
The **See all** link takes you to [Kampp Partners](/en/kampp-partners), with balance,
statement and withdrawals.

## Latest activity

The last six sessions completed by your athletes, with the person's name, the workout and
how long ago it was.

<Note>
  **Earnings report** and **Alert** depend on your plan. On plans that don't include them,
  they simply don't appear and the other cards take up the space.
</Note>

The **Account** section holds **Team** (who else gets into the dashboard), **Settings**
(profile, technical manager, plan and notifications) and the **Help Center**, which is
where you are right now.
