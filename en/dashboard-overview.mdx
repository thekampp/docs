The dashboard is split into simple areas, each handling one part of your work. Here's a
summary to help you find your way.

## The main areas

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Dashboard" icon="chart-line">
    Your business at a glance: active athletes, recent activity and, if you're in the
    partner program, your earnings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Athletes" icon="users">
    Your athlete list. This is where you follow each person, see their progress, leave
    notes and manage access.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Workouts" icon="dumbbell">
    Your workout library, organized in folders. This is where deliveries to athletes and
    teams come from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exercises" icon="list">
    Your exercise collection, with video and instructions — the base of every workout you
    build.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Diets" icon="apple-whole">
    Build meal plans with meals, macros and a water goal, and deliver them to athletes or
    teams.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Teams" icon="user-group">
    Group athletes by cohort, sport or plan. It's also where the team link comes from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Flow" icon="bolt">
    Create automations: messages that go out on their own at the right moments (welcome,
    birthday, athlete gone quiet…).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug">
    Connect WhatsApp and your sales platforms (Kiwify, Hotmart, Sellyo) to deliver workouts
    automatically.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Account

- **Team** — invite other professionals to the dashboard and define what each one can see
  and do.
- **Settings** — your profile, technical manager, **intake forms**, plan and notifications.

<Note>
  The **intake hub** lives under **Settings → Intake**. That's where you build the
  questionnaires and decide when they're required.
</Note>

## Where to go next

If you're just starting, the natural path is: **invite an athlete**, build an **intake
form**, **build the workout** and let delivery happen on its own. Each of those steps has
its own tutorial here.
