The dashboard is split into simple areas, each handling one part of your work. Here's
a quick summary to get you oriented.

## The main areas

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Clients" icon="users">
    Your client list. Here you follow each person, see their progress, leave notes,
    and manage access.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Teams" icon="user-group">
    Group clients by class, sport, or plan. It's also where you generate the invite
    link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Workouts" icon="dumbbell">
    Build workouts, organize your exercise library, and assign programs to clients or
    whole teams.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Assessments" icon="ruler">
    Send intake forms and record physical assessments — measurements, photos, and
    progress history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Kampp Flow" icon="bolt">
    Create automations: messages that go out on their own at the right moments
    (welcome, birthday, inactive client…).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug">
    Connect tools, like your WhatsApp, so the automations can work.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  At the top or in the side menu you'll also find your account settings and your team
  (when you have other professionals helping in the dashboard).
</Note>

## Where to go next

If you're just starting, the natural path is: **invite a client**, then **send an
intake form**, **record an assessment**, and **build the workout**. Each of these
steps has its own tutorial here in the hub.
