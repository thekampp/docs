---
title: "Track the assessment history"
description: "Compare assessments over time and show the client their progress."
---

Every assessment you record becomes a point on the client's timeline. Together, they
tell the story of their progress — and that's gold both for fine-tuning the workout
and for keeping the person motivated.

## How to track it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client">
    In **Clients**, open the profile and go to the **Assessments** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the list of assessments">
    Assessments appear in date order. Click any one to see the recorded details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare progress">
    Follow how the measurements changed from one assessment to the next, and see the
    photos side by side when you recorded them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Showing clients their progress is one of the strongest ways to keep them motivated.
  Use the history in your follow-up conversations.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The more consistent the assessment frequency (every 30, 60, or 90 days, for example),
  the clearer the progress becomes.
</Note>
