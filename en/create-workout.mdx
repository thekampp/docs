---
title: "Create a workout"
description: "Build a workout with exercises, sets, and reps, and choose which folder it goes in."
---

The workout is the heart of your work. Here you build the sequence of exercises with
sets, reps, and loads, and choose which folder it's saved in.

## How to build one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Workouts">
    In the menu, open **Workouts** and click **Create workout**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and choose the folder">
    Name the workout (e.g., "Workout A - Lower body") and choose which **folder** it
    goes in. If you don't pick one, the workout goes to the default folder **My
    Workouts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Choose the workout format: the traditional one (exercise by exercise) or
    circuit/rounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the exercises">
    Search the library and build the list. For each one, set the sets, reps, load or
    time, and rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The workout is saved inside the chosen folder, in your library, ready to be assigned
    as many times as you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Want to create the workout right inside a specific folder? Open the folder's options
  menu (⋯) in your library and use **New workout in folder** — the workout is born in
  the right place.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Can't find an exercise? You can add your own exercises to your library (see "Exercise
  library").
</Note>
