---
title: "Create a workout"
description: "Build a workout with exercises, sets, and reps to send to your clients."
---

The workout is the heart of your work. Here you build the sequence of exercises with
sets, reps, and loads, then send it to a client or a whole team.

## How to build one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Workouts">
    In the menu, open **Workouts** and click **Create workout**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and choose the type">
    Name the workout (e.g., "Workout A - Lower body") and choose the format: the
    traditional one (exercise by exercise) or circuit/rounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the exercises">
    Search the library and build the list. For each one, set the sets, reps, load or
    time, and rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The workout is saved in your library, ready to be assigned as many times as you
    want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Built a workout that fits several people? It stays saved — you can assign it to many
  clients without rebuilding it.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Can't find an exercise? You can add your own exercises to your library (see "Exercise
  library").
</Note>
