---
title: "Share workouts with a link"
description: "Generate a public link and whoever opens it copies your workouts into their own library."
---

Besides assigning a folder to your athletes, you can generate a **public link**. Whoever
opens it copies the workouts into their own account — even if they're not your athlete.

It's how you send a workout to a colleague, promote a free program or hand out a sample of
your method.

## Generate the link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder options">
    In your **Workouts** library, use the folder's options menu (⋯) and choose **Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    The dialog shows the ready-made link. Click the button next to it to copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to whoever you want">
    WhatsApp, a story, a group, your bio — the same link works for as many people as you
    want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the other person gets

Opening the link on a phone, they see a **Copy this workout** button and the Kampp app
opens to add the workouts to their library. People who don't have the app yet are sent to
the App Store or Google Play.

<Note>
  Sharing is not the same as **assigning**. With a share, the person gets a **copy** that
  becomes theirs — you don't track their sessions, and your later edits don't change their
  copy. To coach someone, use [assign workouts](/en/assign-workout).
</Note>

## Tracking and controlling the link

In the same dialog you can see how many people copied it ("3 people copied your workout")
and you get two actions:

- **Deactivate link** — the link stops working right away. Copies already made stay with
  the people who made them.
- **Generate new link** — creates a fresh link after you deactivate one (or when the old
  one expired or hit its limit).

A link can show up as **deactivated**, **expired** or **exhausted** — in every case, just
generate a new one.

<Tip>
  If a link started circulating somewhere you didn't intend, deactivate it and generate
  another. Nobody loses what they already copied, and the old link dies instantly.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Each person can only copy once per link: clicking twice doesn't duplicate the workouts in
  their library.
</Note>
