---
title: "Deliver a diet plan"
description: "Send the meal plan to one athlete or a whole team, with a start date and duration."
---

Once the diet is built, it's time to deliver. You can send it to one person, several, or a
whole team.

## To one or more athletes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the diet's menu">
    In **Diets**, **Templates** tab, use the diet's options menu (⋯).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Assign to athletes">
    Search and tick who should receive it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The diet shows up in their app and starts counting in the **Assigned** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also deliver straight from the person's profile: open the athlete and go to the
  nutrition tab.
</Tip>

## To a team

Open the team under **Teams** and use **Assign diet** in the **Team diets** block. Everyone
on the team gets it — including people who join later.

## Scheduling the delivery

Inside the diet, the **Schedule delivery** block defines the period it's valid:

- **Starts on** — the date the diet appears for the athlete.
- **Duration** — from 1 to 52 weeks, or **No end date**.

With a duration, the diet expires on its own at the end. With no end date, it stays valid
until you replace it.

## Diet statuses

In the **Assigned** tab, each diet shows where it stands:

- **Draft** — still being built.
- **Scheduled** — starts on a future date.
- **Suspended** — paused.
- **Archived** — out of use, but kept.

## What the athlete sees

They follow the day's menu, tick the meals they had and log their water. You see their
adherence in their profile — who's following the plan and who isn't.

<Note>
  Each athlete gets their **own copy** of the plan. That's on purpose: you can adjust one
  person's diet without touching everyone else's. The flip side is that editing the template
  later **doesn't change** diets already delivered — assign it again for that.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If the athlete received diets from more than one coach, they choose which one to follow in
  the app. The most recent choice wins.
</Tip>
