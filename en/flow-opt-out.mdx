---
title: "When a client wants to stop receiving"
description: "Respect opt-outs: mark a client to stop receiving automatic messages."
---

If a client asks to stop receiving automatic WhatsApp messages, it's important to
respect that — both for the relationship and for good data practice. Kampp has a simple
control for this.

## How to opt a client out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the client">
    In **Clients**, open the person's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the WhatsApp opt-out">
    In the profile, use the **WhatsApp** control to mark that the client no longer
    wants to receive automatic messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    From then on, no automation sends WhatsApp to this person. Everyone else keeps
    receiving normally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What if they change their mind?

Just unmark it in the same place — the client starts receiving automatic messages
again.

<Note>
  The opt-out only applies to the **automatic** Kampp Flow messages. You can still talk
  to the client normally, person to person, on their WhatsApp.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Respecting people who asked to leave protects your WhatsApp number's reputation and
  keeps your list healthy — those who receive are the ones who actually want to.
</Tip>
