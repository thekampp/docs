---
title: "When an athlete doesn't want WhatsApp messages"
description: "The athlete controls whether they receive your automatic messages — and automations respect it on their own."
---

Consent belongs to the athlete: they decide whether to receive your automatic WhatsApp
messages. This control lives **in the athlete's own app** — you don't have to do
anything, the automations respect their choice automatically.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="The athlete decides in their app">
    In the app, on the coach screen (under **Permissions / Communication**), the athlete
    has a **"Receive WhatsApp messages"** toggle. They turn it on or off whenever they
    want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automations respect it instantly">
    If the athlete turns it off, Kampp Flow simply **stops sending WhatsApp** to them.
    Everyone else keeps receiving normally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You don't need to do anything">
    There's nothing to toggle in the dashboard — it's all automatic and on the athlete's
    side.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This only applies to the **automatic** Kampp Flow messages. You can still talk to the
  athlete normally, person to person, on their WhatsApp.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Leaving this choice in the athlete's hands protects your WhatsApp number's reputation
  and keeps your list healthy — those who receive are the ones who actually want to.
</Tip>
