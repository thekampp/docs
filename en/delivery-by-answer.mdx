---
title: "Deliver workouts based on the answer"
description: "Each intake answer unlocks the right workout folder, automatically."
---

This is the part that saves the most time: instead of reading every intake form and
delivering the workout by hand, the answer itself decides which folder the athlete gets.

It works like this: you create **deliveries**. Each delivery says *"when the answer is X,
unlock folder Y"*.

## Before you start

You need:

- An intake form with at least one **single choice**, **cards** or **multiple choice**
  question (those are the ones that can be conditions).
- Your workout **folders** already built — one per path. If you don't have them yet, see
  [organize workouts in folders](/en/organize-folders).

## Create a delivery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In **Settings → Intake**, click the form you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Delivery by answer">
    Scroll the builder to the **Delivery by answer (optional)** block and click
    **Add delivery**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the condition">
    Under **When the answer is**, pick the question and then tap the options that apply.
    You can select more than one — any of them counts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the folder">
    Under **Delivers →**, select the workout folder that answer unlocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save intake form**. From now on, whoever answers that way gets that folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Combining conditions (AND)

Inside the same delivery you can use **And also** to stack conditions. The folder is only
unlocked when **all** of them match.

Example: *goal = Hypertrophy* **AND** *trains at the gym* → "Gym Hypertrophy" folder.

<Tip>
  Each question can only appear once per delivery. To handle two different goals, create
  two deliveries.
</Tip>

## Delivery with no condition

If you create a delivery and leave it without conditions, it applies to **everyone who
answers**. That's your baseline — what every athlete gets, no matter what they answer.

Deliveries with conditions **stack on top** of that baseline. So someone who answers
"Hypertrophy" gets the baseline folder **and** the hypertrophy folder.

## When the workout is released

At the top of the block you choose **When to release**:

- **Immediately** — the workout shows up as soon as they finish answering.
- **After the answer** — you set how many **days** later and at what **hour** it opens.

Example: 1 day at 6am means someone answering at 11pm sees the workout at 6am the next
morning. The clock is **theirs**, not yours.

<Tip>
  Holding it for a day gives you time to read the intake form before they start — and it
  builds that nice "tomorrow morning your workout is there" anticipation.
</Tip>

## Warnings the dashboard gives you

The builder flags anything that would behave differently than you expect:

- **"Pick the folder this delivery unlocks"** — with no folder, the delivery is ignored.
- **"Every condition needs at least one option selected"** — an empty condition matches
  nothing.
- **"Another delivery also unlocks this same folder"** — two deliveries pointing at the
  same folder; usually you want different ones.
- **"No condition: this delivery unlocks for everyone"** — a reminder that it's the
  baseline.

<Note>
  Delivery by answer runs **every time** the athlete answers that form — including each
  round of a [recurring form](/en/recurring-intake). Workouts don't duplicate: if they
  already have that folder, nothing is delivered again.
</Note>
