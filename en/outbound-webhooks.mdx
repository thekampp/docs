---
title: "Outbound webhooks (notify another system)"
description: "Have Kampp notify an external automation when a purchase creates access — to send the password over WhatsApp, for example."
---

An **inbound** webhook brings the sale into Kampp. An **outbound** webhook goes the other
way: when access is created, Kampp notifies a system of yours — n8n, Make, Zapier or any
endpoint that can receive data.

The most common use: sending the buyer's login and password over WhatsApp, on top of the
email.

## Create an outbound webhook

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations → Webhooks">
    In the **Integrations** menu, click the **Webhooks** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the webhook">
    Click **New webhook** and give it a name (e.g. "n8n — welcome").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the destination URL">
    Under **Destination URL**, paste the `https://...` address that will receive the event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what triggers it">
    Under **Fire when access is delivered by**, tick the sales integrations (Kiwify,
    Hotmart, Sellyo) that should trigger this webhook.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    After saving, use **Test** on the webhook detail page. Kampp sends a sample event right
    away and shows your system's response.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Kampp sends

An `access.created` event, with:

- the buyer's details (name, email, phone)
- the **login** and **password** for their access
- the name of the integration the sale came from

The request is signed (`X-Kampp-Signature` header) so your system can verify where it came
from — verifying is optional.

## Tracking deliveries

On the webhook detail page, the **Logs** block shows each attempt with date and status. If
your system is down, Kampp retries automatically.

<Note>
  The outbound webhook is secondary: if it fails, **Kampp's own delivery still happens**.
  The buyer never loses access because of an external automation.
</Note>

<Tip>
  With no integration ticked, the webhook never fires. The dashboard warns you when that's
  the case — just click **Edit** and pick one.
</Tip>
