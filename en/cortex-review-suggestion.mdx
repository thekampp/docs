This is the part that's yours. Cortex prepares; **you decide**.

## The suggestion queue

In **Cortex → Suggestions** you see everything in progress, with **Date, Athlete, Type,
Status and Confidence**. Two filters help you find what matters: by **status** and by
**type** (Workout, Diet, Workout + diet).

### What each status means

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Queued** | It's in, hasn't started yet. |
| **Generating** / **Preparing review** | It's being built. Hang on. |
| **Review** | **It's your turn.** |
| **Changes requested** | You asked for changes and they haven't been saved yet. |
| **Reviewed** | You checked it, but haven't delivered it yet. |
| **Approved** / **Scheduled** | Approved, on its way to the athlete. |
| **Delivered** | The athlete already has it. |
| **Failed** | It didn't work out — the reason shows up on the row itself. |
| **Cancelled** | It was discarded. |

<Note>
  The **confidence** percentage tells you how much Cortex was able to lean on what the
  athlete answered. Green is high, amber is medium and **red calls for extra attention** —
  usually because information was missing from the intake form or there's a health point to
  assess.
</Note>

## Opening a suggestion

Click the row. The screen shows:

- **Required points to watch** — an amber card, when there is one. **Always** read it:
  that's where a reported acute symptom, a pregnancy or postpartum, a reported eating
  disorder or a declared health limitation shows up.
- **Suggestion summary** and the **Adapted to what the athlete answered** list — what
  changed and why.
- **Workout** — the program with sessions, exercises, sets and rest times.
- **Diet** — calories, protein, carbs, fat and water, plus the menu meal by meal.
- **What the AI understood** (right-hand column) — the profile and intake form data it used
  as its base. **If something is wrong here, the whole plan deserves suspicion.**
- **History** — the versions, who touched them and when.

<Warning>
  The **Required points to watch** card isn't decoration. It exists precisely to pull your
  clinical eye toward what a rushed review would let slip.
</Warning>

## Adjust before delivering

Click **Edit**. If the suggestion has several parts, Kampp asks **what you want to edit** —
each workout session and the diet open in Kampp's full builder, the same one you already
use.

In there, the save button becomes **Save to suggestion**. Once you save, it moves to
**Reviewed**, with a new version in the history.

<Tip>
  It's worth leaving a note in the **Feedback on the suggestion** field. Besides recording
  why you changed it, it's what makes the next suggestion land closer to what you wanted.
</Tip>

## Approve workout and diet separately

When a suggestion brings **workout and diet**, each card has its own pair of **Approve** /
**Reject** buttons. Both start on "Approve".

That's for the common case of *"the workout came out great, the diet didn't"*: reject only
the diet, approve the workout. The rejected part gets the **Not delivered** badge, becomes
nothing for the athlete and stays in the history. If the athlete already had a diet, they
simply keep it.

## Deliver

Click **Approve and deliver**. Kampp confirms what's about to happen and, once you confirm,
creates the workout and/or the diet in the athlete's account right away.

<Warning>
  **Delivery can't be undone.** Check it before you confirm.
</Warning>

After that the status becomes **Delivered**, a green banner shows what was delivered and a
**View athlete** button appears.

## Discard

If the suggestion isn't any good, use **Discard suggestion**. It leaves the queue, nothing
is delivered and the record stays in the history.

<Note>
  Discarding **doesn't refund the credit** used to generate the suggestion.
</Note>

## Who can do what

Members of your team only see Cortex if they have permission to review prescriptions.
Someone who can review but not approve sees the **Mark as reviewed** button instead of
**Approve and deliver** — the delivery stays with you. See [Permissions](/en/permissions).
