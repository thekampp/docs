---
title: "Create an intake form"
description: "Build a questionnaire with your own questions, preview it, and leave it ready to use."
---

Each intake form is a questionnaire with its own name, and you can have as many as you
want: one for beginners, another for the biweekly check-in, another for your fat loss
crowd.

## Create it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the intake hub">
    In the menu, go to **Settings** and pick the **Intake** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New intake form">
    Choose where to start:

    - **Start from the basic form** — comes with seven ready questions (goal, injuries,
      health conditions, medications, training experience, energy and photos). Just adjust.
    - **Start from scratch** — a blank page, you build everything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a name">
    In **Form name**, write something you'll recognize later (e.g. "Initial intake",
    "Biweekly check-in"). That's the name the athlete sees in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the questions">
    Use **Add question** and pick the type. Each question becomes a card you can edit,
    reorder (↑ ↓ arrows) or remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    On the right you have the **Preview** — exactly what the athlete sees on their phone.
    It updates as you build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save intake form**. It goes into your library already **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  On phones and smaller screens, use the **Edit / Preview** switch at the top to move
  between the builder and the preview.
</Tip>

## What the list shows

In the **Intake** tab, each row under **My intake forms** shows:

- **Name** — plus a note like "in 2 rules" if it's used by a rule.
- **Questions** — how many questions it has.
- **Answers** — how many answers came in. Click the number to see them all.
- **Status** — **Active** or **Inactive**.

The options menu (⋯) on each form gives you **Edit**, **Activate/Deactivate**,
**See answers**, **Export (.xlsx)** and **Delete**.

## Deactivate instead of deleting

Deactivating is reversible: the form stops being required and stops being sent, but the
answers you already collected stay put.

<Note>
  Deleting a form also removes **the rules that use it**. If you just want a break, prefer
  **Deactivate**.
</Note>

<Tip>
  A short form gets answered; a long one gets abandoned. Start with 5 to 8 questions — you
  can ask the rest on the next check-in with a [recurring form](/en/recurring-intake).
</Tip>
