---
title: "Suspend or remove an athlete"
description: "Pause an athlete's access temporarily, or take them off your dashboard."
---

When an athlete stops training with you — for a break or for good — you have two
options: **suspend** (temporary pause) or **remove** (take them off your dashboard).

## The difference between the two

- **Suspend:** the athlete's access is paused, but their history stays saved. Great for
  someone who traveled, took a break, or has an open payment. You can reactivate
  whenever you want.
- **Remove:** takes the athlete off your dashboard. Use it when the relationship has
  truly ended.

## How to do it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the athlete">
    In **Athletes**, open the person's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    Click **Suspend** or **Remove**, depending on the case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm in the window that appears. Done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  When in doubt, **suspend** instead of removing — that way you don't lose the history
  and can reactivate the athlete later with no rework.
</Tip>
