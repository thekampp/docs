---
title: "Engagement triggers (inactive client)"
description: "React automatically when a client goes quiet, loses frequency, or comes back to train."
---

Some of the most valuable triggers are the **engagement** ones — they notice when a
client is cooling off and let you act before you lose them.

## Available triggers

- **No training for X days:** the client hasn't trained for a while.
- **No app open for X days:** they've gone quiet in the app.
- **Frequency dropped:** they used to train more and slowed down.
- **Lost their streak:** they broke that run of consecutive training weeks.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the automation">
    In **Kampp Flow**, create an automation and open the trigger.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the engagement trigger">
    Select, for example, **"No training for X days"** and set the number of days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the action">
    Add the WhatsApp message — something warm, like *"I missed you, {{name}}! Ready to
    get back to it?"*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Re-engagement messages work best when they're kind and pressure-free. The goal is to
  bring the person back, not to nag.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Kampp avoids repeating the same message needlessly. It sends once for that quiet
  spell — it won't keep messaging every day while the client is away.
</Note>
