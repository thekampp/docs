---
title: "Connect the Official WhatsApp (Meta)"
description: "Use the official WhatsApp API, with messages based on approved templates."
---

Kampp also connects with the **official WhatsApp Business API**, from Meta. The main
difference: in automations, the official WhatsApp works with **approved templates** —
message models you register and Meta approves beforehand.

## Before you start

In your Meta account (WhatsApp Manager), you'll need:

- **Phone Number ID** (the number's identifier)
- **Access token** (permanent)
- **WhatsApp Business Account ID** (the "WABA") — **required**, it's what lets Kampp
  fetch your templates.

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    In the menu, open **Integrations** and choose the **Official WhatsApp (Meta)**
    card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new connection">
    Click **New connection**, give it a name, and paste the three details above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The connection becomes active and appears as an option in automations with the
    "WhatsApp (Official)" step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to use it in automations

In the **"Send WhatsApp (Official)"** step, you choose the connection, Kampp **fetches
your approved templates**, and you select one. If the template has blanks (variables),
you fill each one — including **{{name}}** for the client's name.

<Note>
  Templates are created and approved on Meta's side (in Business Manager). Here in Kampp
  you **use** the ones already approved — you don't create templates here.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Not sure which to use? z-API tends to be simpler to start with (free text). The
  official one is best when you already work with Meta's template structure.
</Tip>
