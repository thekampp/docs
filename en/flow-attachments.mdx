---
title: "Attach an image, audio, video, or document"
description: "Send more than text: attach a file to your automatic WhatsApp message."
---

Your automatic message doesn't have to be just text. You can attach an **image**, an
**audio**, a **video**, or a **document** (like a PDF or spreadsheet) — all inside the
WhatsApp action.

## How to attach

<Steps>
  <Step title="In the WhatsApp action, click the paperclip">
    While building the message, click the **paperclip** icon to attach a file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    Pick the image, audio, video, or document from your computer. It's uploaded and
    gets ready in the message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the text (optional)">
    You can send just the file, or file + text together (the text becomes the caption
    of the image, video, or document).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can send

- **Image** (photo, e.g., jpg, png)
- **Video** (mp4 and similar)
- **Audio** (mp3 and similar)
- **Document** (PDF, spreadsheet, Word…)

<Tip>
  Kampp figures out the file type on its own and sends it the right way. You just
  attach it.
</Tip>

<Note>
  There's a size limit per file type (smaller for images, a bit larger for videos and
  audio, larger still for documents). If you go over, the screen warns you right when
  you attach.
</Note>
