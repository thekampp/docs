---
title: "Technical manager"
description: "Register the professional credentials behind your prescriptions — or sign the waiver if you don't have one."
---

The technical manager is the professional who answers for the prescriptions that leave your
dashboard: workouts and meal plans. The credentials sit with the **organization**, not with
your personal profile — so they cover your whole team, even if the person who used the
dashboard leaves later.

You'll find it under **Settings → Technical manager**.

## Filling in the credentials

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    In the menu, go to **Settings** and pick **Technical manager**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the physical education credential">
    The **responsible person's full name** and their **registration number**. They answer for
    workout and cardio prescriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the nutrition credential">
    The **responsible person's full name** and their **registration number**. They answer for
    meal plan prescriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. The footer shows the state: "All saved", "Unsaved changes", or
    what's still missing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  They can be two different people — a physical education professional and a dietitian. You
  need to provide **at least one** of the two.
</Tip>

<Note>
  A credential only counts when it has **both name and number**. Filling in half of it won't
  save: the dashboard highlights the missing field.
</Note>

## If you don't have a professional registration

You can keep using Kampp. To do that, tick the declaration in the **I don't have a
professional registration** block, confirming that you understand your responsibility for
the professional use of the platform's features.

Once saved, the dashboard shows "Declaration confirmed".

<Note>
  Saving requires **a complete credential or the declaration**. It isn't an optional field
  you can leave blank — it's a choice between the two paths.
</Note>

## Who can edit it

Only people with the **settings** permission can edit this. Other team members can view it
but not change it — see [permissions](/en/permissions).
