---
title: "How the athlete answers in the app"
description: "What your athlete sees when they get an intake form — and what happens to their workout."
---

The intake form is answered **inside the app**, on the athlete's phone. It's worth knowing
that screen: it's what you're building when you create the questions.

## What they see

<Steps>
  <Step title="The invite">
    When they open the app, a prompt appears: **"You have a new intake form"** (with your
    name, when you have a coach profile set up). The button says **Answer now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="One question per screen">
    Questions come one at a time, with a counter ("3 of 8") and a **Continue** button. On
    the last one, the button becomes **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The completion screen">
    Once done, they see "Intake form submitted" — or the
    [completion screen you built](/en/intake-completion-screen), with your video and offer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Details that matter

- **Required questions** block them from moving forward until answered.
- **Photos** can be taken on the spot or picked from the gallery.
- The prompt **never opens during a workout** — Kampp waits for the session to end.
- If they close it halfway, the invite comes back later.

## And the workout?

If the form came from a **rule** (required), the workouts you delivered stay locked until
they answer. After submitting, the workout is released the next morning at 6am in their
timezone.

If you set up [delivery by answer](/en/delivery-by-answer), that's the moment the right
folder is unlocked for them.

<Tip>
  Want to see how it turned out? Send the form to yourself (or a test account) and answer
  it in the app — it's the fastest way to see the real thing, video and all.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Older intake records, from the previous format, are still visible in the athlete's
  profile marked as a **previous record**. They don't disappear — they just aren't edited
  there anymore.
</Note>
