---
title: "Let the athlete fill in the assessment"
description: "Generate a link, choose which measurements they report and get the data straight into your dashboard."
---

Not every assessment has to be done by you. When the athlete lives in another city, or
when the measurements are simple enough for them to take alone, you send a **link** — they
fill it in from their phone, **with no login**, and the data lands in your dashboard.

## Generate the link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the athlete's assessments">
    Athlete → **Assessments** tab → **Physical assessments** sub-tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send to the athlete">
    This opens the **Assessment links for the athlete** window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate first link">
    After the first one, the button becomes **New link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what they fill in">
    Kampp already ticks the essentials (weight, body fat %, waist, hip, chest, arm, thigh
    and the notes). Tick or untick whatever you want, field by field or by whole section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set how long it lasts">
    Under **Link validity**, choose how many days it works for — from 1 to 60, with 14 as
    the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate it">
    Click **Generate link**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Ask for little. A link with 6 fields comes back filled in; one with 30 fields gets
  abandoned halfway. You can always send another one later.
</Tip>

## Send it to the athlete

On the link card you get:

- the **full address**, with a copy button;
- the green **WhatsApp** button, which opens the conversation with the message already
  written and the link pasted in;
- a button to open the link in a new tab, so you can check what they'll see.

## Keep track

Each link shows a status badge:

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting to be filled** | Sent, not answered yet. |
| **Athlete filled it in** | The data is already in your dashboard. |
| **Expired** | The deadline passed. Generate a new one. |
| **Revoked** | You disabled the link. |

When they answer, the assessment joins the list as usual, with the **Athlete filled it in**
badge so you know those numbers weren't measured by you.

## Change or cancel

- **Edit fields** — changes what the athlete needs to fill in. The link and whatever they
  already answered are preserved; they see the new fields when they reload.
- **Revoke** — kills access right away. Use it when you sent it to the wrong person or you
  no longer want that data collected.

<Warning>
  Revoking takes effect immediately and can't be undone. If you need it again, generate
  another link.
</Warning>

## What the athlete sees

A simple page with your photo and your name — *"[your name] asked you to fill this in"* —,
the expiry date, a counter of how many fields they've answered and only the fields you
ticked.

When they submit, they read **Got it, we received your answers!** and can come back to the
same link to fix something while it hasn't expired.

If something's wrong, they get a clear explanation: **Invalid link**, **Expired link** or
**Revoked link** — always pointing them to ask you for a new one.
