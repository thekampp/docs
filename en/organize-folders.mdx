---
title: "Organize workouts into folders"
description: "Use folders to tidy your workouts: create, rename, move, delete, and share."
---

Every workout lives inside a **folder**. Folders keep your library organized and are the
basis for delivering workouts: you always assign, copy, and share by folder.

The **My Workouts** folder is the default folder — it's where the workouts you haven't
placed in any other folder live.

## In the coach panel

In your **Workouts** library, folders appear as lists you can open and close. Each folder
has an options menu (⋯) with everything you need.

### Create a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workouts">
    In the menu, go to **Workouts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the folder">
    Use **New folder** and give it a name (e.g., "Beginners", "Hypertrophy", "Phase 1").
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Folder actions

In each folder's options menu (⋯) you'll find:

- **Assign folder** — delivers the folder's workouts to an athlete, several athletes, or a team.
- **Share** — generates a [public link](/en/share-workout-link); whoever opens it copies all
  the folder's workouts into their account.
- **New workout in folder** — creates a workout right inside that folder.
- **Rename folder** — changes the folder's name.
- **Delete folder** — removes the folder.

### Move a workout to another folder

Want to move a workout? Open the workout (or its menu) and use **Move to folder**,
choosing the destination.

<Tip>
  Organize by goal or by training phase. Well-arranged folders make it easier to build a
  periodized program, since each phase uses a folder.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Deleting a folder **does not delete the workouts**. They stay saved and go back to
  **My programs**.
</Note>

## In the athlete app

The athlete also sees their workouts organized in folders that open and close. The
default folder is called **My Workouts**.

The athlete can:

- **Create, rename, and delete** their own folders.
- **Reorder** the folders and **reorder the workouts** inside a folder by holding and
  dragging.

<Note>
  The workouts you deliver show up for the athlete ready to do, without allowing edits.
  The athlete organizes them, but doesn't change what came from you.
</Note>
