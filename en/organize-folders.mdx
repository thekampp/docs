---
title: "Organize workouts into folders"
description: "Use folders to tidy your workouts: create, rename, move, delete, and share."
---

Every workout lives inside a **folder**. Folders keep your library organized and are the
basis for delivering workouts: you always assign, copy, and share by folder.

The **My Workouts** folder is the default folder — it's where the workouts you haven't
placed in any other folder live.

## In the coach panel

In your **Workouts** library, folders appear as lists you can open and close. Each folder
has an options menu (⋯) with everything you need.

### Create a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workouts">
    In the menu, go to **Workouts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the folder">
    Use **New folder** and give it a name (e.g., "Beginners", "Hypertrophy", "Phase 1").
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Folder actions

In each folder's options menu (⋯) you'll find:

- **Assign folder** — delivers the folder's workouts to an athlete, several athletes, or a team.
- **Train with this one** — sends the folder to **your** workouts in the app, for you to train
  yourself. It doesn't affect your athletes.
- **Share** — generates a [public link](/en/share-workout-link); whoever opens it copies all
  the folder's workouts into their account.
- **New workout in folder** — creates a workout right inside that folder.
- **Rename folder** — changes the folder's name.
- **Delete folder** — removes the folder.

<Note>
  **Assign**, **Share** and **Train with this one** only show up when the folder has at least
  one workout inside.
</Note>

### Train with your own workouts

**Train with this one** is a switch: once it's on, the item becomes **Remove from my app** and
the folder gets the green **In your app** tag in its header.

<Tip>
  It's the fastest way to use Kampp for yourself, without signing up as your own athlete.
</Tip>

### Change the order of the workouts

When a folder has more than one workout, the **↑ ↓** arrows show up on each row. They change
the order inside the folder.

<Note>
  The order you set **also applies to the copies already delivered** to athletes. In other
  words, tidying the sequence here tidies it for whoever already received it.
</Note>

### Move a workout to another folder

In the options menu (⋯) **of the workout** in the listing, use **Move to folder** and choose
the destination (a folder or **My programs**).

<Warning>
  If you try to delete a workout that is assigned to someone, Kampp warns you that it's in use
  and says to how many athletes. Check before confirming.
</Warning>

### Block folders are separate

The **Blocks** tab has its **own folder list**. A folder created inside Blocks doesn't show up
on the workouts tab, and vice versa. See [Blocks](/en/blocks-before-after).

<Tip>
  Organize by goal or by training phase. Well-arranged folders make it easier to build a
  periodized program, since each phase uses a folder.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Deleting a folder **does not delete the workouts**. They stay saved and go back to
  **My programs** — which is how the group with no folder shows up in the library listing (in
  the builder, that same destination is called **My Workouts**).
</Note>

## Folders in the athlete's profile

In an athlete's **Workouts** tab, what they received also shows up **grouped by folder** —
both the folders exclusive to them and the ones that came from your library.

There you have **New folder** to create a folder just for that athlete and, inside it, **New
workout**.

<Warning>
  On that screen the **trash icon is in the folder header**, and deleting the folder removes
  **its workouts** from that athlete. To just move a workout somewhere else, use **Move to
  folder**.
</Warning>

## In the athlete app

The athlete also sees their workouts organized in folders that open and close. The
default folder is called **My Workouts**.

The athlete can:

- **Create, rename, and delete** their own folders.
- **Reorder** the folders and **reorder the workouts** inside a folder by holding and
  dragging.

<Note>
  The workouts you deliver show up for the athlete ready to do, without allowing edits.
  The athlete organizes them, but doesn't change what came from you.
</Note>
