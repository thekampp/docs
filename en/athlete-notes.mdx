---
title: "Take notes about an athlete"
description: "Keep important observations for each athlete so nothing slips through."
---

Notes are your little notebook inside the athlete's profile. Use them to record what
you don't want to forget: an injury, a preference, a conversation, an agreement.

## How to add a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the athlete">
    In **Athletes**, open the person's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to notes">
    In the **Overview** tab, the **Notes** card sits in the right-hand column, next to the
    calendar. Click **+ Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the category">
    There are four: **General** (the default), **Performance**, **Health** and **Goals**.
    The category becomes a colored tag on the note and helps you find it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write">
    The field takes up to **2,000 characters**, with a counter underneath.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Done. The note is stored with its date, in that person's profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Long notes show up truncated in the list, with **See more** to open them. Writing plenty
  is no problem.
</Tip>

## Edit and delete

Hover over the note to reveal the **pencil** (edit) and **trash** (delete) icons.

<Warning>
  When you delete, Kampp asks for confirmation — and there's no going back: the note is
  removed for good.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Notes are internal — the athlete doesn't see them. They're for you and your team to
  stay organized.
</Note>
