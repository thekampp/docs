---
title: "Take notes about an athlete"
description: "Keep important observations for each athlete so nothing slips through."
---

Notes are your little notebook inside the athlete's profile. Use them to record what
you don't want to forget: an injury, a preference, a conversation, an agreement.

## How to add a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the athlete">
    In **Athletes**, open the person's profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to notes">
    Find the **Notes** area and click **Add note**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and categorize">
    Write what you need and, if you like, pick a category (for example: general,
    injury, goal). This makes it easier to find later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Done. The note is stored in the profile, visible only to your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can **edit** or **remove** a note at any time.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Notes are internal — the athlete doesn't see them. They're for you and your team to
  stay organized.
</Note>
