---
title: "Create a team"
description: "Organize your athletes into teams by class, sport, or plan."
---

**Teams** are how you organize your athletes into groups. You can have a team per
class, per sport, per subscription plan — however makes sense for your work. The
invite link also comes from the team.

## How to create one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Teams">
    In the menu, open **Teams** and click **Create team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a name">
    Choose a clear name so you can find it later (e.g., "Morning class",
    "Jiu-Jitsu", "Premium plan").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Done. The team shows up in your list and is ready to receive athletes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What now?

With the team created, you can:

- **Invite athletes** with the team link (see "Invite an athlete to your team").
- **Assign a workout** to the whole team at once.
- Use the team as an **automation trigger** in Kampp Flow (e.g., send a welcome when
  someone joins this team).

<Tip>
  No need to create a thousand teams. Start with the groups you actually use — you can
  always create more later.
</Tip>
