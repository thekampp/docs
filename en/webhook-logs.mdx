---
title: "Track incoming events (logs)"
description: "See every sale your platform sent to Kampp, what Kampp did with it, and how to reprocess a failure."
---

Every notification your sales platform sends to Kampp is recorded. It's the best place to
answer "this person bought — why didn't they get it?".

## Where to look

In **Integrations**, open the platform and then the integration. The **Recent logs** block
lists the events, newest first, with:

- **Date** it arrived
- **Event** — Paid, Refunded, Chargeback, Canceled, Expired
- **Action** — Access granted, Access revoked or Ignored
- **Email** of the buyer
- **Status** of the processing

Click any row to open **Event details**, with the raw content the platform sent.

## What each status means

- **Received** — it arrived and is queued.
- **Processing** — Kampp is working on it.
- **Processed** — all good.
- **Ignored** — the event needs no action (e.g. bank slip generated, pending payment,
  rejected sale).
- **Duplicate** — the same notification arrived again; Kampp doesn't do it twice.
- **Failed** — something blocked the processing. The reason is in the details.

## Reprocess a failure

When an event shows up as **Failed**, open the details and use **Reprocess**. Kampp runs
the same event again with the data it already has.

<Tip>
  Reprocessing is safe: if access was already granted, nothing is duplicated.
</Tip>

## Common problems

- **Nothing shows in the logs** — the URL isn't pasted in the platform, it's incomplete, or
  the event wasn't ticked there.
- **It says "Ignored"** — the event isn't an approved purchase (it's a bank slip, pending
  or rejected). That's expected behavior.
- **They bought but got no workout** — access was granted, but delivery depends on the
  intake form. Check that there's a rule tied to that webhook and that the form has
  [deliveries by answer](/en/delivery-by-answer) set up.
- **No buyer email** — without an email Kampp can't create the account. Check the record in
  the sales platform.

<Note>
  The log keeps the original content sent by the platform. It's your proof of what arrived
  and when — handy when you need to check a sale with their support.
</Note>
