---
title: "View a client's profile and progress"
description: "Follow everything about a client in one place: workouts, assessments, and progress."
---

When you open a client, you see their full profile — your at-a-glance view of that
person. Everything that matters for follow-up is gathered there.

## What you'll find in the profile

- **Details and contact:** name, email, phone (with a WhatsApp shortcut), and how long
  they've been your client.
- **Timeline:** the activity history — completed workouts, assessments, milestones.
- **Progress:** progress charts, like weight and performance over time.
- **Assessments:** intake form and recorded physical assessments.
- **Workouts:** what's assigned and what's already been completed.

## How to open it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Clients">
    Open **Clients** in the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the client">
    Tap the person's name to open the full profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the tabs">
    Use the tabs to switch between timeline, progress, assessments, and workouts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the WhatsApp shortcut in the profile to message the client in one tap, without
  leaving the dashboard.
</Tip>
