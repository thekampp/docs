---
title: "Tags: workout tags and athlete tags"
description: "There are two kinds of tag in Kampp, and they don't mix. Here's what each one does."
---

The word "tag" shows up in two places in Kampp and means different things. It's worth
knowing the difference before you count on it.

## Workout tags

They're for **categorizing your workouts**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workout">
    In the builder, look for the **Workout tags** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one or create one">
    Search for an existing tag or type a new name and click **Create "..."**. It's created
    on the spot and already selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You have two lists:

- **System tags** — the ones that come ready from Kampp, the same for everyone.
- **My tags** — the ones you created.

Tags appear on the workout and on its detail screen.

<Note>
  Today workout tags are a **visual label**: there's no filter by tag in the library, and no
  screen to rename, recolor or delete a tag once it's created. Create them carefully,
  because the name sticks.
</Note>

## Athlete tags

They're for **grouping people**, and they're used as the audience for the
[chat broadcast](/en/chat-broadcast).

<Warning>
  **You can't tag an athlete by hand.** There's no such button on their profile or in the
  athlete list. Athlete tags are only born inside an automation.
</Warning>

### How to create an athlete tag

It happens in [Kampp Flow](/en/flow-what-is), as a step in the automation:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build a flow">
    Choose the trigger that defines the group (for example: the athlete became at risk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Tag step">
    On the **+** button, pick **Tag**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    **Add tag** or **Remove tag**, and write the name (for example: `At risk`).
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on, every athlete who hits that trigger gets the tag on their own — and the tag
starts showing up in the broadcast selector.

<Note>
  That's why the broadcast's **Tag** selector can be empty: if none of your flows tag
  anyone, there are no athlete tags yet. Build the flow first.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The ready-made Flow templates already do this: one marks `At risk` when the athlete cools
  off, and another removes the tag when they start training again. It's the fastest way to
  have a group that maintains itself.
</Tip>

## The two never cross

Workout tags and athlete tags are separate universes. Even with identical names, one never
shows up in place of the other.

| | Workout tag | Athlete tag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where you create it** | In the workout builder | In a Kampp Flow step |
| **What it's for** | Organizing your library | Defining a broadcast audience |
| **Apply by hand?** | Yes | No — only through automation |
