---
title: "Add and import athletes"
description: "Bring your athletes into the dashboard, one by one or several at once."
---

Besides the invite link, you can also bring your athletes in directly from the
dashboard — handy when you already have a list of people.

## Add an athlete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Athletes">
    In the menu, open **Athletes** and click **Add** (or **Import**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Fill in the athlete's name and contact (like email or phone). The more complete,
    the easier to follow up later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Save. The athlete joins your list, and you can already send intake forms,
    assessments, and workouts to them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite several at once

Have a list of emails? Use **Import athletes**: just **paste the emails** all at once —
separated by comma, space, or one per line. Kampp creates/invites them all together,
without you typing one by one.

<Note>
  How many you can import at once depends on your plan's available seats. The screen
  shows how many seats you have before you confirm.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Prefer the athlete to sign up themselves? Use the **team invite link** — they join
  on their own through the app. See the "Invite an athlete to your team" tutorial.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Adding an athlete here doesn't send them anything automatically. You control what to
  send and when (intake, workout, messages).
</Note>
