---
title: "Add and import athletes"
description: "Bring your athletes into the dashboard, one by one or several at once."
---

Besides the invite link, you can also bring your athletes in directly from the
dashboard — handy when you already have a list of people.

## Add an athlete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Athletes">
    In the menu, open **Athletes** and click **Add** (or **Import**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Fill in the athlete's name and contact (like email or phone). The more complete,
    the easier to follow up later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Save. The athlete joins your list, and you can already send intake forms,
    assessments, and workouts to them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import a spreadsheet

Have a list of athletes? In **Athletes**, click **Import**. You can use your own
spreadsheet (.xlsx, .xls, or .csv), no template needed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the spreadsheet">
    Drag the file in or click to choose it. The first row must be the header (the
    column names).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the columns">
    Kampp detects the name, last name, email, and phone columns on its own. If
    something is off, just pick the right column. Name and email are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the options">
    Optionally, put everyone in a **delivery** and set a **default password**. Without
    a default password, each athlete gets a random one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and import">
    The review shows how many are ready and which rows are left out (no email, invalid
    email, or duplicate), and you can download those rows.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After importing:

- people who **didn't have a Kampp account** get one and receive their login and password by email;
- people who **already had an account** get an invite to accept in the app;
- people who **were already your athletes** stay as they are.

The import runs gradually so the emails get delivered well. You can follow the
progress on screen and leave the page: the import keeps going. At the end, you can
download a report with the result of each row.

<Note>
  How many you can import depends on your plan's available seats. The screen shows how
  many seats you have before you confirm.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Prefer the athlete to sign up themselves? Use the **team invite link** — they join
  on their own through the app. See the "Invite an athlete to your team" tutorial.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The athlete only receives access (login and password, or the invite). Intake forms,
  workouts, and messages go out the way you set up your deliveries and forms.
</Note>
