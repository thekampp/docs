When you open an athlete you land on their profile — your x-ray of that person.

Go to **Athletes** in the menu and click the name.

## The header

At the top you get the photo, the name, the **email**, the **phone** (click it to open
WhatsApp), the date of birth and how long they've been your athlete.

Next to the name, the **status of the relationship**:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | All good, they're training with you. |
| **Invite pending** | You invited them, but they haven't accepted yet. |
| **Suspended** | You paused their access to workouts. |
| **Deactivated** / **Expired** | The relationship has ended. |

### The buttons at the top

- **Chat** — opens the [chat](/en/chat-talk-to-athlete) with that person.
- **Suspend** / **Reactivate** — cuts off or restores access to workouts.
- **Resend invite** — takes the place of the previous button while the invite is pending.
- **Remove** — takes the athlete out of your roster.

<Warning>
  **Removing is irreversible.** That person's history is erased for good. If you only want
  to pause, use **Suspend**. See
  [Suspend or remove an athlete](/en/suspend-remove-athlete).
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Profile looks empty and you don't understand why?** Check the status. With an **invite
  pending**, the profile, the metrics and the workouts don't show up until the person
  accepts. Use **Resend invite** if they say they never got it.
</Note>

## The seven tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The summary. It's where you land. |
| **Workouts** | What they received, what they performed and their training profile. |
| **Diet** | The meal plan and adherence. See [Track nutrition](/en/track-nutrition). |
| **Metrics** | The numbers behind their progress. See [Athlete metrics](/en/athlete-metrics). |
| **Assessments** | Intake form, feedback, physical assessment, Body Scan and consent. |
| **Payments** | Their finances with you. See [Athlete payments](/en/athlete-payments). |
| **Timeline** | The full history. See [Athlete timeline](/en/athlete-timeline). |

## The Overview tab

### The four numbers at the top

<Steps>
  <Step title="Engagement">
    A score from **0 to 100** that sums up whether the athlete is following the plan, with
    the label **Excellent**, **Good**, **Attention** or **Critical**.

    Anyone who joined less than two weeks ago shows as **New athlete · settling in** and
    doesn't get a score yet — it's an adaptation period, there's nothing to judge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Completed workouts">
    How many they finished **in the current month**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Weekly frequency">
    The average of workouts per week over the **last 4 weeks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Last workout">
    When it was — "Today", "Yesterday", "N days ago" or the date — and which workout it was.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Calendar

Shows the month with the days they trained marked. Use the arrows to move between months.

### Insights

Short sentences Kampp puts together on its own from their behavior, with the alerts first.
Things like *Inactive for more than 2 weeks*, *Slowed down this week*, *Never performed a
workout* and *Low completion rate* — or the positive ones, like *N days in a row* and
*Excellent consistency*.

<Tip>
  Insights are the shortcut to the question "who do I talk to today?". Open the profile,
  read the first two lines and you already know whether you need to act.
</Tip>

### Notes

Your little notebook about that person, in the right-hand column. See
[Notes](/en/athlete-notes).

## The training profile (in the Workouts tab)

In the **Workouts** tab, in the right-hand column, sits the **Athlete profile** card —
everything they filled in during the app onboarding, in blocks you open and close:

- **Restrictions** — limitations and injuries they declared (knee, lower back, shoulder…).
  **Read this before prescribing.**
- **Physical** — gender, age, height, weight, belt/grade.
- **Sport and goal** — sport, level, time practicing, goal, experience.
- **Training plan** — where they train, weekly workout target, time available, preferred
  time of day, gym access.
- **Available equipment** — what they have on hand.
- **Kampp stats** — workouts, total time, volume, streak and level.

If they never completed the onboarding, the card shows **Not filled in**.

<Note>
  On accounts without a higher plan, the profile warns that you're seeing **only the last
  30 days**, with a shortcut to unlock the full history.
</Note>
