**Kampp Partners** is the referral program: you refer, the person you referred subscribes,
and you get a recurring percentage.

## Where it lives

On the **Dashboard**, in the **Earnings report** card, click **See all**. That opens the
**Kampp Partners** screen.

<Note>
  This isn't your athletes' finances. What your athletes pay you lives in the
  [Athlete payments](/en/athlete-payments) tab.
</Note>

## The four numbers up top

| Card | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Available balance** | What you can already withdraw. |
| **Pending balance** | What has been generated but is still in the holding period. |
| **Total earned** | Everything you've accumulated in the program. |
| **Total withdrawn** | What has already gone out to you. |

## Your tier

The **Your tier** card shows the percentage you get **on the net revenue of your referrals**,
a progress bar and how many active paying users are left until the next tier.

<Warning>
  Your tier can **drop**. If you lose active paying users, an orange **grace period** notice
  shows up: you keep your current percentage for a few more days to recover. After that, the
  percentage goes down.
</Warning>

## Referred customers

Shows how many active paying users you have, split between **Athletes** and **Coaches**, and
the **earnings window** — how long that referral keeps paying you (lifetime or a set number
of months).

## Statement

The table lists every movement with **Date, Movement and Amount** (green for money in, red
for money out), and shows the **next release**.

You'll see entries like commission generated, commission released, commission reversal,
refund adjustment and payout requested.

## Withdrawing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your payout account">
    Before your first withdrawal, go to **Settings → Payout account** and fill in your
    details. You can get paid by **PIX** (national ID, email, phone or random key) or by
    **international bank transfer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Withdraw">
    At the top of the Kampp Partners screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Kampp shows how much is available. **The minimum is R$ 100.00 per withdrawal.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Request payout**. It goes into the queue and you follow it in the **Payouts**
    card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the **Payouts** card you see **Date, Method, Status and Amount**. The status goes through
**Under review**, **Processing** and **Paid** (or **Declined** / **Canceled**).

<Note>
  The **Withdraw** button stays disabled while your available balance is below the minimum.
</Note>

## Not part of it yet?

If you're not in the program, the screen shows the invitation to join and start referring.
