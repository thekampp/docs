---
title: "Create your account"
description: "Create your coach account in a few minutes, by email or with Google/Apple."
---

To use the dashboard, you need a coach account. There are two ways, and both lead to
the same place — pick whichever is easiest.

## Create with email and password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up screen">
    On the sign-in screen, click **Create an account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter your name, email, and create a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Done. Your coach area is set up automatically and you land right in the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create with Google or Apple

Prefer not to create a password? Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with
Apple** — the account is created instantly, with nothing to type. (It's the same
account you'd use in the app, if you already use Google/Apple there.)

<Note>
  Depending on the case, you may be asked to **confirm your email** with a link we
  send. Just open the email and click to verify.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Already had a client account in the app? When you sign in as a coach, your account is
  upgraded — you don't lose anything or create a duplicate.
</Tip>
