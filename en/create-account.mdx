---
title: "Create your account"
description: "Create your coach account in a few minutes, by email or with Google/Apple."
---

To use the dashboard, you need a coach account. There are two ways, and both lead to
the same place — pick whichever is easiest.

## Create with email and password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-up screen">
    On the sign-in screen, click **Create an account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter your name, email, and create a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Done. Your coach area is set up automatically and you land right in the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create with Google or Apple

Prefer not to create a password? Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with
Apple** — the account is created instantly, with nothing to type. (It's the same
account you'd use in the app, if you already use Google/Apple there.)

## Confirm your email

You may be asked to **verify your email**. Kampp sends a **6-digit code** that you type on
the **Verify your email** screen.

Didn't get it? Use **Resend code** — it allows a new send every 60 seconds. Check your spam
folder before trying again.

## Already had an athlete account in the app?

No problem: it's the **same account**, which now also gets the coach dashboard. You don't
lose anything and nothing is duplicated.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up with the same email">
    Kampp spots the existing account and asks whether you want to turn it into a coach
    account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your password">
    It's the password you already use in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your team">
    Enter your team or gym name and, if you want, a contact phone. Click **Activate coach
    account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you created your athlete account with **Google or Apple**, this path isn't available
  yet — talk to Kampp support in that case.
</Note>
