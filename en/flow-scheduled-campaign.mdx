---
title: "Scheduled campaign (specific date)"
description: "Schedule a message to go out to all your athletes on a set date."
---

Want to send an announcement to everyone on a specific date — a year-end promo, a
holiday note, the launch of a new class? The **scheduled campaign** does exactly that:
it fires once, on the date and time you set.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the automation">
    In **Kampp Flow**, create an automation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date trigger">
    In the trigger, select **"Scheduled campaign (specific date)"** and choose the
    **date and time** to fire.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Add the WhatsApp message (and an attachment, if you want). Use **{{name}}** to
    personalize, even when it's for everyone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate and wait for the date">
    Leave the automation on. When the date arrives, Kampp sends it to your athletes
    automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set the campaign up in advance and leave it on. You don't need to be online at the
  time — Kampp fires it on its own when the date arrives.
</Tip>

<Note>
  It's a **one-time** send: after the campaign goes out, it ends (it won't keep
  repeating). For a new date, create a new campaign.
</Note>
