---
title: "Recover your password"
description: "Forgot your password? Get back in with a few steps."
---

Forgetting your password happens. Recovering it is quick, and you do it yourself, no
help needed.

## How to recover it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password?">
    On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot password?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Type your account email and confirm. We send a recovery link to that email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the email and set a new password">
    Open the message that arrived, click the link, and set a new password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in as usual">
    Done. Go back to the sign-in screen and log in with the new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder and make sure you typed the right
  address. If needed, request the link again.
</Tip>

<Note>
  If you sign in with **Google** or **Apple**, there's no password to recover — just
  use the "Continue with Google/Apple" button on the sign-in screen.
</Note>
