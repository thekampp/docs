---
title: "Connect Kiwify"
description: "Link your Kiwify products to Kampp so access is delivered as soon as the sale is approved."
---

With Kiwify connected, every paid order creates the buyer's account in Kampp and triggers
delivery. Refunds remove access.

## In Kampp: create the integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In the menu, go to **Integrations** and click the **Kiwify** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new integration">
    Click **New Integration** and give it a name you'll recognize later (e.g. "Kiwify —
    Annual Plan"). If you sell different products, create one integration per product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the token (optional)">
    In the **Token** field you can paste your Kiwify webhook token. It lets Kampp confirm
    the notification really came from Kiwify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the buyers' password">
    Leave **Set a default password** off and each buyer gets a random password. Turn it on
    and everyone gets the same password you type (at least 6 characters).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm refund and chargeback">
    **Revoke on refund** and **Revoke on chargeback** come switched on. Turn them off if you
    prefer to handle those cases by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and copy the URL">
    Click **Create Integration**. The next screen shows the **Webhook URL** — copy it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## In Kiwify: paste the URL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open notifications">
    In the Kiwify dashboard, go to **Settings → Notifications → Webhook URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the Kampp URL">
    Paste the URL you copied and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the events">
    Enable at least:

    - **Order paid** (`order_paid`) — this is what unlocks access.
    - **Refund** (`order_refunded`) — this is what removes it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test it

Make a test purchase (or use a recent real order). Then open the integration in Kampp:
under **Recent logs** you'll see the sale arriving, with the buyer's email and the
**Access granted** tag.

If nothing shows up, check that the whole URL was pasted and that the event is ticked in
Kiwify. See also [track incoming events](/en/webhook-logs).

<Tip>
  One integration per product keeps things clear: you can require a different intake form
  per offer and see the sales separated in the logs.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Creating the integration alone doesn't deliver any workout yet. What the buyer receives is
  decided by the intake form — see
  [deliver workouts based on the answer](/en/delivery-by-answer).
</Note>
