---
title: "Assign workouts to an athlete or team"
description: "Deliver a folder of workouts to one person, to several athletes, or to a whole team."
---

Workouts are delivered by **folder**: you pick a folder and send it to whoever will
train. That way, all the workouts in that folder land in the athlete's account at once.

## Assign a folder to an athlete

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the athlete">
    In **Athletes**, open the person who will receive the workouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the folder">
    Select the **folder** with the workouts you want to deliver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to deliver it">
    - **Assign folder** — delivers the folder's workouts to the athlete. They receive and
      train; you stay the owner of the workouts.
    - **Copy folder** — creates copies exclusive to this athlete. You can tweak these
      workouts just for them, without touching your originals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Done. The workouts show up in the athlete's app, ready for them to do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use **Assign folder** when the same plan fits several people. Use **Copy folder** when
  you want to personalize the workouts for that specific athlete.
</Tip>

## Assign to a team or several athletes

To send the same workouts to many people at once, it's also by folder:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the folder">
    In your **Workouts** library, find the folder you want to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Assign folder">
    In the folder's options menu (⋯), choose **Assign folder** and pick the
    destination: a whole **team** or **several athletes** at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Everyone receives the folder's workouts in the app, all at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Assigning a folder to a **team** is the fastest way to send the same plan to several
  people at the same time.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Among the destinations there's also **Everyone** — it reaches your entire roster. Kampp
  shows an amber warning when you pick that option, precisely because there's no easy way
  back.
</Warning>

## Deliver only to who fits (teams)

When the destination is a **team**, three filters show up. They exist so you can send a
single folder and each person receives the right version.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Who is this workout for?">
    **Any**, **Male** or **Female**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where does the athlete train?">
    **Any**, **At home**, **Gym** or **Mixed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What's the athlete's goal?">
    **Any**, a specific goal (Hypertrophy, Fat loss, Strength, General
    conditioning, Better stamina, Strong grip, Explosiveness, Mobility, Strong core, Active
    recovery) or **Default · everyone else**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The difference that matters:

| Option | How it behaves |
| --- | --- |
| **Any** | **It stacks.** It applies to everyone, and still adds up with the specific deliveries. |
| **A specific goal** | Only people who picked that goal in the app receive it. |
| **Default · everyone else** | **Exclusive.** It's the safety net: it catches whoever didn't match any specific delivery. |

<Tip>
  Anyone who hasn't picked a goal yet (or hasn't said where they train) **receives it as soon
  as they set it** in the app. You don't have to redo the delivery afterwards.
</Tip>

## Schedule the delivery and set a deadline

In the same modal there's the **Schedule delivery** switch. With it on, you define:

- **Workout duration** — **Unlimited** or from **1 to 52 weeks**. That's what generates the
  **Expires** column and the duration in weeks you later see in the athlete's profile.
- **When to release** — **On a set date**, or an individual countdown from each athlete's
  entry.
- **Release after** and **Release time** — for the individual countdown.
- **Start date** — when the plan kicks in.

<Note>
  The individual countdown is what makes a folder work as a journey: whoever joins today gets
  week 1 today, and whoever joins next month gets week 1 next month.
</Note>

## Share a folder by link

Want to deliver a folder to someone who is **not** on your list? Use the folder's options
menu (⋯) and choose **Share**: whoever opens the link copies the workouts into their own
account.

See the step by step in [share workouts with a link](/en/share-workout-link).

## After you deliver

In the athlete's **Workouts** tab, what they received shows up **grouped by folder**, with
the **Start** date, the date it **Expires** (or "No end date"), the status and the duration
in weeks.

<Note>
  As the athlete trains, their completions come back to you with load, reps, duration and how
  hard they found it. See [Completed workouts](/en/completed-workouts).
</Note>
