---
title: "Assign a workout to a client or team"
description: "Send a ready-made workout to one person or to a whole team at once."
---

Once you've built a workout, it's time to put it in the client's hands. You can assign
it to a specific person or to a whole team at once.

## How to assign it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the workout">
    In **Workouts**, find the workout you want to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign it to whoever will train">
    Use the **assign** option and choose the destination: a specific client or a whole
    team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Done. The workout shows up in the client's app, ready for them to do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Assigning to a **team** is the fastest way to send the same workout to several people
  at the same time.
</Tip>

<Note>
  As the client trains, their completions come back to you — you can follow what was
  done in the person's profile.
</Note>
