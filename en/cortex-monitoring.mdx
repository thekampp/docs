---
title: "Monitoring: who needs you right now"
description: "Kampp watches the workouts you delivered and tells you when an athlete deserves your attention."
---

With 80 athletes on your roster, it's impossible to look at everyone's progress every
week. The **Monitoring** tab does that sweep for you.

## Where to find it

Menu **Cortex** → **Monitoring** tab, in the **Next best actions** card.

Each row shows the athlete, the type of recommendation, the **reason** and the
**recommended action**.

## The alerts you might see

| Alert | What it usually means |
| --- | --- |
| **Consider progressing the load** | The athlete is handling it with room to spare — you can go up. |
| **Review the workout load** | Something doesn't add up: the workout may be too heavy. |
| **Reach back out** | They went quiet. Worth a message before it turns into a cancellation. |
| **Ask for feedback** | There isn't enough information for you to see how it's going. |

## What to do with each row

- **Acknowledge** — you've seen it and you'll act. It leaves the list and the menu counter
  drops.
- **Dismiss** — it doesn't make sense for this athlete. It disappears from the list.

<Tip>
  "Acknowledge" and "Dismiss" change nothing in the athlete's account — they're just your
  inbox. What actually solves things is what you do next: send the message, adjust the
  load, ask for the assessment.
</Tip>

## An empty list is a good sign

When there's nothing, Kampp shows **No intervention needed right now**. Kampp only looks at
the workouts you delivered and only opens a recommendation when it finds enough evidence —
it would rather stay quiet than bury you in false alarms.

<Note>
  Want to act straight from there? Open the conversation in [Chat](/en/chat-talk-to-athlete)
  or let the message go out on its own with
  [Kampp Flow](/en/flow-engagement-triggers).
</Note>
