Assigning a folder to a **team** delivers it to everyone at once — including people who
join later. It's the team board: if you're on the team, you get it.

## Assign a folder to the team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team">
    In **Teams**, click the team and go to the **Team workouts** block.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Assign folder">
    Pick the folder with the workouts the team will receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define who gets it">
    Use the filters (below) to target the delivery, or leave everything on **Any** for
    everyone to receive it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it (optional)">
    Set when the workouts become available and for how many weeks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filters: delivering the right workout

You can assign several folders to the same team, each for a different profile. Three
filters decide who gets each one:

### Who is this workout for?

**Any**, **Male** or **Female**.

### Where the athlete trains

**Any**, **At home**, **Gym** or **Mixed**. People who haven't said where they train yet
receive it as soon as they fill in their profile.

### The athlete's goal

Here's an important difference between three options:

- **Any** — everyone receives it, whatever their goal. It **stacks** with goal-specific
  folders (someone chasing hypertrophy gets the "Any" folder **and** the hypertrophy one).
  Use it for the baseline that applies to everybody.
- **A specific goal** (Hypertrophy, Fat loss, Strength…) — only people who picked that goal
  in the app receive it.
- **Default · everyone else** — the safety net: it goes to people who didn't match any
  specific goal on that team. It never comes together with a specific one.

<Tip>
  The recipe that works: an **Any** folder with the essentials (mobility, core),
  goal-specific folders for the goals you serve, and a **Default · everyone else** folder so
  nobody ends up without a workout.
</Tip>

## When the workout shows up

In the scheduling step you choose between releasing it right away, on a set date, or a
while after the person joins the team. That last one is the most used for cohorts: each
athlete's clock starts on the day they joined, not the day you set it up.

## Team diets

The **Team diets** block works the same way: **Assign diet** delivers the plan to the whole
group. See [deliver a diet plan](/en/assign-diet).

<Note>
  People who join the team later automatically receive what's on the board — no need to
  assign again.
</Note>
