---
title: "Connect WhatsApp (z-API)"
description: "Link your WhatsApp to Kampp via z-API to send messages in automations."
---

For Kampp Flow to send WhatsApp messages, you need to connect your WhatsApp once. The
most flexible way is through **z-API**, which lets you send free-text messages, with or
without attachments.

## Before you start

You'll need a z-API account, where you create an "instance" of your WhatsApp. From
there, you copy three pieces of information:

- **Instance ID**
- **Instance Token**
- **Athlete-Token** (from the account security area)

## How to connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    In the menu, open **Integrations** and choose the **WhatsApp (z-API)** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new connection">
    Click **New connection** and give it a name you'll recognize later (e.g., "Main
    WhatsApp").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the credentials">
    Fill in the Instance ID, Instance Token, and Athlete-Token you copied from z-API.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Done. The connection becomes active and shows up as a **"Send via"** option in your
    automations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can have more than one WhatsApp connection (e.g., different numbers) and choose
  which one to send from in each automation.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Your credentials are stored securely and never shown again — only the last digits, so
  you can tell which is which.
</Note>
