---
title: "Manage a team"
description: "Rename, track members, and share your team's link."
---

After creating a team, it's inside it that you look after the group: see who's there,
share the invite, and keep everything organized.

## What you can do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team">
    In **Teams**, click the team you want to manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the members">
    The list shows every client in that team. From there you can open each person's
    profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the invite">
    Use **Copy invite link** to bring in more clients. The same link works for as many
    people as you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the team">
    You can rename the team and organize who belongs to it as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A client can be in more than one team at the same time — handy when they take part
  in two sports, for example.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Changing the team doesn't delete the client's history. Their assessments and
  workouts stay saved in their profile.
</Note>
