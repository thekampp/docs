title: "Create your first automation"
description: "Build a welcome automation from scratch, step by step."
---

Let's build a classic automation together: **when a client joins the team, they get a
welcome message on WhatsApp.** The same path works for any other automation.

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Kampp Flow">
    In the menu, go to **Kampp Flow** and click **Create automation** (or pick a
    ready-made template to start faster).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the trigger">
    Click the trigger and select **"Athlete joined a team"**. If you want, pick a
    specific team — or leave it for any team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the action">
    Click **Add** and choose **Send WhatsApp**. Write the message, for example:
    *"Hi {{name}}, welcome to the team! Let's train 💪"*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the automation on">
    When you're happy, **activate** it. From then on, every new client in the team gets
    the message automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The {{name}} trick

Wherever you write **{{name}}**, Kampp swaps it for the client's name when sending. So
each person gets a personalized message, even though it's automatic.

<Tip>
  The automation saves itself as you build. And you can leave it off while testing,
  turning it on only when it's ready.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Want more than one step? You can chain them: welcome now, wait 3 days, and send
  another message. See "Waits and sequences".
</Note>
