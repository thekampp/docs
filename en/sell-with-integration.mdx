---
title: "Sell and deliver workouts automatically"
description: "Connect your checkout to Kampp: the sale creates the buyer's account, sends their access and delivers the right workout."
---

You sell your program on Kiwify, Hotmart or Sellyo. When a sale is approved, Kampp can do
the rest on its own: create the buyer's account, email their access, ask for the intake
form and deliver the workout that fits their profile.

It all lives in the **Integrations** menu.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You create an integration in Kampp">
    An integration is the channel between the sales platform and Kampp. When you create
    one, you get a unique **webhook URL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You paste that URL in the sales platform">
    That's how you tell it "let Kampp know when someone buys". Each platform has its own
    path — see the tutorial for yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The sale happens">
    When the payment is approved, the platform notifies Kampp. If the buyer doesn't have an
    account yet, it's created on the spot and they get an email with their login and
    password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The intake form steps in">
    If you created an intake rule for that integration, the buyer has to answer before the
    workout unlocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The right workout is delivered">
    Their answer decides which folder they get — fat loss, hypertrophy, beginner, whatever
    you built.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why route it through the intake form

Because that's what turns a sale into actual coaching. Instead of every buyer getting the
same PDF, each one answers three or four questions and receives the program that makes
sense for them — without you opening the dashboard.

To set this up, see [deliver workouts based on the answer](/en/delivery-by-answer) and
[when to require the intake](/en/recurring-intake) (the **Webhook** option).

## What the buyer gets

- An **email** with where to download the app, their login (the email used in the purchase)
  and their password.
- The password can be **random** (a different one per buyer) or **a default password of
  yours**, set on the integration.
- Buyers who **already had an account** keep their own password — the new access is just
  added on top.

## Refunds and chargebacks

On the integration you decide whether access is removed when the platform reports a
**refund** or a **chargeback**. Both are on by default.

<Note>
  Removing access **doesn't delete the buyer's account** or their workout history — it only
  takes away the content that purchase unlocked.
</Note>
