---
title: "Sign in to your account"
description: "Access the dashboard with email and password, or your Google or Apple account."
---

You can sign in whichever way is easiest for you. There are three options, and they
all lead to the same account.

## Ways to sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email and password">
    Enter the email and password you signed up with and click **Sign in**. Forgot
    your password? Use **Forgot password?** to recover it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue with Google">
    Click **Continue with Google** and pick your account. Done — no password to type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue with Apple">
    Click **Continue with Apple** and confirm with your Apple ID. It works the same
    way.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you use the Kampp mobile app with Google or Apple, it's the **same account**
  here in the dashboard. You don't create anything new — you go straight in.
</Tip>

<Note>
  First time signing in as a coach? When you log in, your coach area is set up
  automatically. You land right in the dashboard, ready to go.
</Note>
