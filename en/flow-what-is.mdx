**Kampp Flow** is your automatic-message assistant. You set it up once, and it takes
care of sending the right message, to the right person, at the right time — without you
having to remember anything.

## The idea in one sentence

> **When X happens, do Y.**

For example: *when an athlete joins the team, send a welcome message on WhatsApp.* You
define this once and Kampp does it on its own, forever.

## How an automation is built

Every automation has a simple sequence:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger — what kicks it off">
    The event that starts the automation. E.g.: the athlete joined the team, did their
    first workout, went quiet for a few days, has a birthday today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Condition (optional) — a filter">
    An extra detail to refine it. E.g.: only if the athlete is in a certain team, only
    if their frequency is low.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait (optional) — give it some time">
    Pause before the next step. E.g.: wait 3 days, or wait until a date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Action — what Kampp does">
    What actually happens. Today: send a WhatsApp message or tag the athlete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Examples you can build

- **Welcome:** athlete joins the team → send a welcome message.
- **Inactive athlete:** went X days without training → send an "I missed you".
- **Birthday:** on the athlete's birthday → send a happy birthday.
- **Campaign:** on a set date → send an announcement to everyone.

<Note>
  For WhatsApp messages to work, you need to connect your WhatsApp once in
  **Integrations**. See the tutorials in that section.
</Note>
