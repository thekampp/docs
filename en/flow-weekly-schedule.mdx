---
title: "Weekly message (and WhatsApp only for those who didn't read)"
description: "Send every Saturday, or every day, at the same time to all athletes, and send WhatsApp only to those who didn't read the chat."
---

With the **Agenda que se repete** (repeating schedule) trigger, every athlete receives the
message together, on the same day and time, every week, no matter when each one joined.
Great for "how was your week?".

## Example: Saturday feedback

```
Repeating schedule: Saturday at 09:00
  → Send chat message: "How was your week? Workouts, diet, sleep..."
  → Wait 4 hours
  → Condition: Chat read status (didn't read the last message)
  → Send WhatsApp (Official)
```

Whoever read the chat stops at the condition. Only those who didn't read get the WhatsApp.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the trigger">
    Under **Data e agenda**, choose **Agenda que se repete**. Select **Toda semana** (every
    week) and the days (more than one is fine), or **Todo dia** (every day). Then pick the time
    (Brasília time).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter who receives it (optional)">
    Use the usual conditions: **Delivery**, **Tag**, **Team**... Want a different message for
    each delivery? Create one flow per delivery (the **Duplicate** button helps).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the sequence">
    Chat message, a wait and, after it, the **Leitura do chat** (chat read status) condition.
    Finally, the WhatsApp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Your **active athletes** who pass the flow's conditions receive it.
- Each athlete receives it **at most once a day** per flow. Changing the time after it fired
  doesn't send again on the same day.
- If you activate the flow up to 15 minutes after the time, it fires right away. After that,
  it waits for the next selected day.

## "Chat read status" condition

Checks whether the athlete read the **last message** that you, your team or a flow sent in the chat.

- **Didn't read the last message**: passes those who haven't opened it yet.
- **Read the last message**: passes those who saw it, meaning the message shows the two ticks in the chat. Opening the conversation is enough; no reply needed.
- An athlete with no message from you in the chat passes neither.

It can sit at the start of the flow (right after the trigger) or **in the middle of the
sequence**, after a wait. In the middle, it's checked when the flow reaches it. If the athlete
doesn't pass, the flow stops there (what was already sent stays sent).

<Tip>
  To place the condition in the middle, add the message and the wait first. Then click **+**
  and choose **Condição** (Condition): it goes at the end of the sequence.
</Tip>
