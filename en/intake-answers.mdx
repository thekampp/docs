---
title: "See and export the answers"
description: "Track everything your athletes answered and download a spreadsheet with the results."
---

Every submitted answer is stored — per athlete and per round. You can look at it two ways:
by form (everyone who answered that questionnaire) or by athlete (everything that person
has answered).

## All answers for one form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the intake hub">
    Go to **Settings → Intake**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the answer count">
    In the **My intake forms** list, the **Answers** column shows how many came in. Click
    the number to open the answers screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also get there from the form's options menu (⋯), under **See answers**.

## Download the spreadsheet

In the form's options menu (⋯), choose **Export (.xlsx)**. The file includes:

- The athlete's name, email and phone
- The round and the date they answered
- One column per question in the form

<Tip>
  The spreadsheet is great for segmenting: filter by goal and you instantly have the list
  of everyone chasing fat loss for a specific campaign.
</Tip>

## One athlete's answers

In the athlete's profile, **Assessments** tab, the **Intake** block lists all their forms.
Click any answered one to see the answers and the photos they uploaded.

<Note>
  Answers include health data. Handle the export with the same care — avoid sending the
  spreadsheet through open channels.
</Note>
