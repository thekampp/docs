---
title: "Intake question types"
description: "Every component you can use: text, choices, scale, slider, photos, pause screens and image carousel."
---

When you click **Add question** in the builder, you pick the component type. Each one
solves something different — here's what they do and when to use them.

## Questions

### Free text

A text box for the athlete to write whatever they want. Use it for injuries, history,
"what brought you here".

### Number

Numbers only. Good for age, weight, time available per session.

### Single choice

A list of options where the athlete picks **one**. It's the most used type — and one of
the few that can drive workout delivery.

### Cards (button choice)

Same as single choice, but the options show up as **large buttons** in the app. Looks
better and is faster to tap when options are short (e.g. "Home", "Gym").

### Multiple choice

The athlete can pick **several** options. Use it for "which days do you train", "what
equipment do you have".

### Linear scale

A rating ruler (e.g. 1 to 10) with labels at each end. You define:

- **Minimum** and **Maximum**
- **Minimum label** and **Maximum label** (e.g. "Low" and "High")
- **Scale color** — whether a high score is good (green at the top), a high score is bad
  (red at the top) or neutral (no judgement, just a blue ramp)

Perfect for energy, pain, sleep quality, stress.

### Slider

A sliding bar. Besides minimum and maximum, you can set:

- **Unit** (e.g. cm, kg, min, %)
- **Starting value** — where the slider begins

Good for weight, height, available minutes — anything with a wide range.

### Photos (front / side / back)

Asks for assessment photos. You choose which angles you want: **Front**, **Side** and
**Back** (one, two or all three).

## Screens that aren't questions

### Interstitial (pause screen)

A screen with just a title and some text — no field to fill in. Use it to give a breather
mid-form ("Great! Now let's talk about your training") or to explain why you're about to
ask something sensitive.

### Image carousel

Up to **6 images** the athlete swipes through. Use it to show your method, social proof,
or to explain a choice visually before asking about it.

## Settings that work on any question

### Subtitle

Small text under the question title. Use it to instruct: *"If yes, describe it. If not,
write 'None'."*

### Helper text per option

On choice questions, each option can have an extra line of explanation. Great for telling
similar options apart ("Mixed — I train at home and at the gym").

### Conditional text field

Tick **Show a text field when certain answers are selected** and choose which options
reveal it. The classic case: they pick "Yes, I have an injury" and only then the "Which
injury?" field appears.

You set the **label**, the **placeholder** and whether the answer is **required**.

### Required answer

Turns on the lock: without answering that question, the athlete can't move forward.

### Order

Use the ↑ ↓ arrows on the question card to change its position. The athlete answers in
the order you build, one question per screen.

<Tip>
  Only **Single choice**, **Cards** and **Multiple choice** can be used as conditions in
  [delivery by answer](/en/delivery-by-answer) and as the key question of the
  [completion screen](/en/intake-completion-screen). If you want to route workouts by goal,
  ask the goal with one of those three types.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Carousel images and the photos the athlete uploads are stored securely — the photos show
  up in their profile, next to the answers.
</Note>
