---
title: "When to require the intake (and repeat it every X weeks)"
description: "Create rules to ask new athletes, everyone, or buyers — once or on a schedule."
---

An intake form on its own goes nowhere: what sends it to the athlete is a **rule**. The
rule answers two questions — **who** has to fill it in and **how often**.

Rules live in **Settings → Intake**, in the **When to require** block.

## Create a rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New rule">
    In the **When to require** block, click **New rule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the form">
    Under **Which intake form**, select the questionnaire this rule will ask for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who it applies to">
    You have three options:

    - **New athletes** — only people who join from now on.
    - **All athletes** — every current athlete gets the form now, and new ones too.
    - **Webhook** — only people who buy through a specific integration (Kiwify, Hotmart,
      Sellyo). You pick which webhook next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the repetition">
    Tick **Repeat every** and enter the number of weeks. Leave it off and the form is asked
    **once only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule">
    Click **Save rule**. It takes effect right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A check-in every 15 days

This is the most requested setup. Build a short follow-up form (weight, energy, pain,
adherence, photos) and create a rule like this:

- **Which intake form:** Biweekly check-in
- **Applies to:** All athletes
- **Repeat every:** 2 weeks

Every two weeks the athlete gets the questionnaire again, answers in the app, and you see
the progress answer by answer.

<Tip>
  The countdown starts **from the last answer**, not from the day you created the rule.
  Someone who answered today only gets it again in two weeks — someone who never answered
  still has the current round pending.
</Tip>

## How rounds work

Each time the form is asked, that's a **round**. The next round is only created once the
previous one was **answered** and the deadline passed — nobody piles up three pending
questionnaires because they went on holiday.

Each round stores its answers separately, so you can compare January's check-in with
March's without losing anything.

## What the rule does to the workout

A form that comes from a rule is **required**: while it's pending, the workouts you
delivered to that athlete stay locked. As soon as they answer, the workout is released
(the next morning, at 6am their time).

<Note>
  This applies to the **first** round and every one after it. If you use recurrence, tell
  your athletes — that's what keeps check-ins on schedule.
</Note>

## Managing rules

In the list, each rule shows the form name and two tags: **who** (New athletes / All
athletes / Purchase · webhook name) and **how often** (Once / Every N weeks).

- Use **Edit** to change anything.
- Untick **Active** to pause the rule without deleting it.
- The trash icon removes the rule (answers you already received stay).

<Tip>
  One form can be used by several rules at once — for example, the same initial intake
  required from new athletes **and** from Kiwify buyers.
</Tip>
