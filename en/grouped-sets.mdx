---
title: "Superset, tri-set and grouped set"
description: "Link exercises the athlete does back to back, with no rest in between."
---

When two or more exercises are done back to back, you **group** them in the builder. The
athlete sees the exercises joined by a bracket and understands right away that they're meant
to be done in sequence.

<Note>
  Grouping only exists in the **Classic workout** format. In a **Rounds workout** it makes no
  sense — the round is already a circuit.
</Note>

## How to group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Put the exercises in the right order">
    Grouping is always **with the exercise above**. Use the ↑ ↓ arrows to get the order right
    first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover over the exercise below">
    The action icons show up on the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the chain icon">
    The tooltip says **Superset with the one above**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To undo it, click the same icon — now it shows as a broken chain, in red, with **Undo
superset**.

## The name changes with the size

You don't choose the label: it comes from the size of the group.

| Exercises in the group | How it shows up |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | **Superset** |
| 3 | **Tri-set** |
| 4 or more | **Grouped set** |

To build a tri-set, group the second with the first and then the third with the second.

## How it looks on screen

The exercises in the group sit inside a red frame, with the label on top. **The same bracket
shows up on the workout detail screen** and in the athlete's app, following the same naming
rule.

<Tip>
  The rest that matters is the one on the **last** exercise of the group — that's what
  separates one pass from the next. On the exercises in the middle, leave the rest at zero so
  the athlete really goes straight through.
</Tip>

## A rule that catches everyone

If you **move** or **delete** an exercise and the group is left with only one, **the grouping
undoes itself**. That's not a bug: a superset of one exercise doesn't exist.

After reorganizing exercises in a workout with supersets, it's worth checking that the
brackets are still where you wanted them.
