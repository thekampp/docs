---
title: "Team link: whoever joins becomes your athlete"
description: "Share one link and people join your team straight from the app, already as your athletes."
---

Every team has its own **link**. Anyone who opens that link and joins the team
automatically becomes your athlete — no manual registration on your side.

It's the fastest way to bring people in: post the link on a story, in a WhatsApp group or
in your bio, and people join on their own.

## Where to find the link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the team">
    In the menu, go to **Teams** and click the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Team link">
    In the information block, look for **Team link** — "Share it: whoever joins becomes your
    athlete automatically".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and share">
    Use the copy button next to the link. The same link works for as many people as you
    want, with no expiry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What people see

Opening the link, they get a page with the team name, your name, the description, how many
workouts the team has and how many people are already in. Then:

- **On a phone** — the **Open in app** button takes them straight to Kampp. If they don't
  have the app, the link sends them to the store (App Store or Google Play).
- **On a computer** — the page explains they need to download the app on their phone to
  continue.

Once they join, they show up in your **Athletes** list and immediately receive whatever the
team delivers.

<Tip>
  A good cover image makes a difference: it appears on this page and in the app. Set it
  under **Team cover**, in the **Information** block.
</Tip>

## What they get on joining

Everything on the team board: workouts and diets assigned to the team. If you used gender,
training location or goal filters, they only get what matches their profile — see
[team workouts](/en/team-workouts).

<Note>
  The link doesn't expire and has no limit on people. If you want to close entries, you'll
  need to remove the team or ask support to generate a new code.
</Note>
